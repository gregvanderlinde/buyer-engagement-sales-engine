# Fit Chat -- Free

Obey HOW_WE_HELP.md and FIT_CHAT_PASTE.txt hard limits. Concepts and outcomes only.

## How to run

1. Invite: ask if they want to see whether this would help them.
2. Ask a few short questions (role, what they sell, what hurts).
3. Reflect their situation in their words; mark guesses as questions.
4. Say honestly: fit / maybe / not a fit -- and why at concept level.
5. Prompt once: "Does this sound like it would help you -- or what would need to be true?"
6. If fit: Free preview browsing or one small next step they choose -- not a method dump.
7. If not fit: say so and stop selling.

## Never

- No IP dump, no proprietary process, no internal architecture
- No prices invented (say pricing being set)
- No fake ROI or case studies
- No auto-send offers
- Drafts and advice only
