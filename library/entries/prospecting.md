# Prospecting
*Turn suspects into prospective buyers so the funnel stays full.*

## Executive summary

Prospecting creates opportunities and drives sales. It is how you uncover potential deals and keep a healthy pipeline. Not everyone is a prospect, but everyone starts as a suspect. Research, outreach, and clarifying questions turn cold leads into warm ones -- and only then does the Buyer Engagement Process begin.

## Bottom line

Prospecting is not optional. Continuously develop suspects into prospective buyers so you sustain and grow business instead of waiting for inbound luck.

- Fix, avoid, achieve as the bridge from interest to need.
