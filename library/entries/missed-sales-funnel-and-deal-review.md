# Missed Sales Funnel and Deal Review
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on missed sales funnel and deal review.*

## Executive summary

Missed opportunities create inaccurate revenue projections. Sellers must realistically project sales opportunities using the Buyer Engagement Forecast process, and develop an accurate sales forecast projection process by taking part in proactive coaching sessions with sales leaders. Missed sales forecast and deal review Proactive sales leaders that consistently hold deal review sessions are able to effectively coach sellers to avoid overstating sales revenue and can accurately predict opportunities that will close, reducing occurrences of lost revenue.

## Bottom line

Missed opportunities create inaccurate revenue projections.
