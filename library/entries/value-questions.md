# Value Questions
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on value questions.*

## Executive summary

uncover what is truly important to the buyer in terms of both personal value (specifically to the buyer) and business value (for the buying organization). They seek to uncover the personal and business feelings, values, and attitudes of buyers and the buying organization. Sellers that uncover this information will be in line to offer a solution that meets the criteria of both the buyer and the buying organization. Sellers that understand value in the proper context and ask effective will be able to provide value on both a personal and business level, which makes it easier to engage buyers and align to their needs.
