# Unique Capability Match
*Ground the buyer's unique issues to what only you can do.*

## Executive summary

A Unique Capability Match is the connection between the buyer's unique issues and your Unique Capabilities. It is built from Business Objectives, prioritized projects, concerns to overcome, and the buyer's Current Solution -- then differentiated so the buyer can justify why your offer, not a generic alternative.

## Bottom line

Uncover Business Issues with disciplined questions. Differentiate. Ground their issues to your Unique Capabilities. Until that grounding is real, you do not have a match -- you have a pitch.

## In this entry

- What Unique Capability Match is (and is not).
- The factors that build it: objectives, projects, concerns, current solution, unique capabilities.
- Questioning pattern: wide open, deepen, validate.
- How it feeds Qualified Outcomes Match.
