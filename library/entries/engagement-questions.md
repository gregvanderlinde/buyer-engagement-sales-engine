# Engagement Questions
*Ask for action -- engagement questions test commitment and move the deal forward.*

## Executive summary

Engagement requires alignment. Once you are aligned, you are in a position to ask for commitment
actions. A buyer's willingness to act tells you their real interest. If you cannot get a commitment
to action, be wary -- and seriously consider disengaging so you can spend time where buyers will help
build the solution.

## Bottom line

Engagement questions evoke action. They set up give and take: you negotiate an action in exchange for
something you provide. Use them at the end of every meeting, whenever you need to confirm position,
and to estimate how many steps remain. Ask for the right size of commitment -- too much too soon
makes buyers step back.

## In this entry

- The purpose of engagement questions (gauge, commit, build momentum).
- Why they reveal true intentions better than assumptions.
- When to use them -- and what to do when the buyer will not act.
- How to recover: re-align, ask again, find the root cause, or walk.
