# Does a Buyer-Centric Approach Make a Difference?
*Research on aligning to the buyer's purchase process.*

## Executive summary

Industry research indicates that in most sales organizations, about half of sellers need to improve their understanding of the buyer's purchasing process. Organizations that diligently follow a buyer-centric sales process increase results by more than 5% year over year. Performance improves when selling starts from the buyer's perspective.

## Bottom line

Organizations that adopt a buyer-centric sales process improve sales results by about 10-15%.
