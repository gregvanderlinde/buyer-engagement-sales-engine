# What Is the Impact of Strategic Action Plans?
*Research on written strategic action plans and execution.*

## Executive summary

Less than 10% of companies believe they excel at developing strategic action plans. Approximately 60% of companies believe they need to improve their ability to develop strategic plans for their key accounts in order to win more deals. Strategic planning helps identify ideal buyers.

## Bottom line

Top organizations strategically plan their business initiatives. This leads to better execution, effective differentiation from competitors, and the development of longer term business relationships with their buyers.
