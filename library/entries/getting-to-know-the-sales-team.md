# Getting to Know the Sales Team
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on getting to know the sales team.*

## Executive summary

Many new sales leaders do not take the time to adequately know their sales team. Getting to know the sellers will help the sales department perform at its best. Only by learning the strengths, weaknesses, and potential of each seller can the sales leader lead their department to reach its peak performance level. Sales leaders that take the time and planning to understand their sales team will be in a strong position to use the strengths of their best sellers as well as shore up any gaps that be be present.

## Bottom line

Many new sales leaders do not take the time to adequately know their sales team.
