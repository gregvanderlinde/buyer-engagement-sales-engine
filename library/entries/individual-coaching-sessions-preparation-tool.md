# Individual Coaching Sessions Preparation Tool
*A prep sheet sales leaders use before one-on-one coaching so the session stays specific, balanced, and useful.*

## Executive summary

Individual coaching sessions work best when sales leaders prepare with a simple tool that frames the seller's goals, gaps, and next commitments before the meeting starts.

## Bottom line

Review notes from recent one-on-ones, decide what to communicate, lead with specific positive feedback, name negative behavior with situation and detail, and decide what you can delegate.
