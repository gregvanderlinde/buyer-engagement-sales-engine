# Does Account Planning Affect Revenue from Current Accounts?
*Research on account planning and prospecting tactics versus expansion revenue.*

## Executive summary

60% of revenue comes from existing accounts. Approximately two-thirds of companies believe they are leaving money on the table and need to increase revenue from existing accounts. Sellers who are proficient at selling to existing accounts have a 10% better quota achievement rate over those who are not. Top companies use effective planning tactics to maximize revenue in existing accounts. There is a direct correlation between a company's ability to meet or exceed expectations with their account planning and their ability to generate additional revenue from existing buyers.

## Bottom line

Sellers must be proficient at account planning and prospect development in order to achieve revenue targets.
