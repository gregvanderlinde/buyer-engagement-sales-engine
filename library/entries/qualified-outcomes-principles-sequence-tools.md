# Qualified Outcomes -- Principles, Sequence, and Tools
*Universal principles, a clear sequence, and a small toolset -- the Module 1 spine.*

## Executive summary

Qualified Outcomes rests on three legs: universal principles about how people change and choose, a sequence that builds a match before you ask for a deal, and tools that keep every call on the same track. Learn the three legs and you can coach yourself mid-conversation instead of hoping the pitch lands.

## Bottom line

Principles explain why buyers move. The sequence tells you what to build next. The tools (form, prompter, cheat sheets, question banks) make both visible on the call and in the account plan.

## In this entry

- The six principles that drive change and choice.
- The sequence from Business Objectives through Unique Capabilities to proof.
- The toolset and how each piece is used (territory, pre-call, talk track, review).
- Benefits of running the process as a team system.
