# CPRD Personality Profiling -- An Overview
*A sales-floor read of how people like to engage -- Closers, Prospectors, Relationship, and Detail as blends, not boxes.*

## Executive summary

CPRD helps you rapidly recognize how a person prefers to engage so you can match style for clarity and rapport. It sits on two simple scales -- introvert vs extrovert, and task vs process -- and yields four style tendencies: Closer, Prospector, Relationship, and Detail. Most people are a mix; you read what they show and flex toward it. Used well, CPRD climbs the Trust Ladder faster and earns deeper questions sooner -- never as manipulation.

## Bottom line

Match how someone likes to engage, not just what they care about. Seat and role say what matters; CPRD says how to say it.
