# Money is a Short Term Fix- Motivate Reps the Right Way
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on money is a short term fix- motivate reps the right way.*

## Executive summary

Sales leaders who wish to temporarily solve motivational issues use monetary rewards. However, long term behavioral change is created by challenging sellers, offering praise, recognition, and the opportunity to grow and expand their sales career. Money is a Short Term Fix: Motivate sellers the Right Way While money motivates activity, having the right culture has the greatest impact on increasing motivation. Money is the easy fix; however, it often does not produce the sales leader's intended result. Focusing on motivation tactics beyond offering money has the largest impact on seller performance and their ability to generate greater revenue more often.

## Bottom line

Sales leaders who wish to temporarily solve motivational issues use monetary rewards.
