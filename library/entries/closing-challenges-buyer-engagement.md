# Closing Challenges and the Buyer Engagement Process
*Buyers have tactics too -- stay aligned so the close is a natural step, not a forced event.*

## Executive summary

Buyers are savvy. Their purchase process and tactics create real closing challenges. When you
understand those tactics and stay inside the Buyer Engagement Process, you are far less likely to
concede too early or treat the close as a surprise. The best close is the one that does not feel
like a close at all -- it is the natural next step of an effectively negotiated, mutually engaged deal.

## Bottom line

Do not force the close, and do not let it surprise anyone. Closing is the end of progressive
commitment, not the first ask. Know the buyer's purchasing ability and tactics so you protect
alignment and a real win/win instead of folding under pressure.

- How informed buyers buy -- and the pressures they carry.
