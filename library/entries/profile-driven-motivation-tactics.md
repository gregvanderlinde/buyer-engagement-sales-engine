# Profile Driven Motivation Tactics
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on profile driven motivation tactics.*

## Executive summary

For sellers, the internal process of self-motivation is driven by the incentives provided by their company. Most sellers crave a balance of achievement, recognition, challenge, reward, and progression. They are also motivated by more practical needs such as job and financial security. By developing profiles on their sellers that outline their personal and professional goals, needs, and desires, sales leaders can develop customized incentive programs (this is an add on and is very different to the commission structure) that will motivate sellers to perform at their peak level. (See: Profiling sellers) As sales leaders learn to use different skills and techniques to understand and motivate their sellers, they will be more successful in leading a high-performing sales team.
