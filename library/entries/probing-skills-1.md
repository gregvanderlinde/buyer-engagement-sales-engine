*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on probing skills 1.*

## Executive summary

The art of asking questions is key to your success as a salesperson. Since the object of the exercise is to gather as much information as you can that will eventually lead to the sale, information is good.

## Bottom line

Spend your best energy framing the right questions; strong probes uncover the real problem before you sell a solution.
