# Qualified Outcomes Match
*The Numbers x The Win, tied to a real capability match.*

## Executive summary

Qualified Outcomes Match is where the Unique Capability Match meets the outcomes that matter: The Numbers (business result) and The Win (personal). People make personal decisions with corporate budget for corporate reasons. You quantify both, bring Key Stakeholders in, and agree how you will prove it.

## Bottom line

Business impact without personal stakes stalls. Personal stakes without business impact will not survive scrutiny. Keep returning to both; they are the fuel and the scoreboard.
