# Does Lead Development Make a Difference?
*Research on developing leads before handoff to sellers.*

## Executive summary

More than fifty percent of companies are not satisfied with the ability of their sellers to generate leads and turn them into effective sales interactions. Approximately 40% of initial sales interactions develop into a sales cycle. Top organizations that successfully turn leads into sales interactions.

## Bottom line

Organizations successfully convert leads into sales interactions through pre call planning, alignment with sales and marketing initiatives, and effective lead nurturing.
