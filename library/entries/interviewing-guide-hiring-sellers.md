# Interviewing Guide for Hiring Sellers
*A systematic interview process so sales leaders compare candidates on evidence, not gut feel.*

## Executive summary

Hiring the right seller matters for results and for the cost of getting it wrong. This guide gives sales leaders a repeatable way to prepare, open, question, and close interviews, then score candidates. Questions are organized across Introductory, Sales, Experience, and Personality perspectives, with a preference for behavioral questions that cover situation, objective, action, and result.

## Bottom line

Use one structured interview process, ask behavioral questions, and score every candidate the same way before you hire.

## In this entry

- How to prepare and open the interview.
- Why behavioral questioning beats closed questions.
- The four question perspectives with sample prompts.
- What to do after the interview, including Candidate Evaluation.
