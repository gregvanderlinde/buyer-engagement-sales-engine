# Five Types of Closing
*Closing is the natural next step of a well-handled process -- not a trick at the end.*

## Executive summary

Without a close there is no deal, but manipulative closing tactics damage relationships and create buyer's remorse. Five legitimate techniques -- assumptive, summary, alternative, question, and final-objection -- let you ask for commitment when alignment and value are already present. Used at the right time, they secure revenue without putting the partnership at risk.

## Bottom line

Close when the buyer is engaged and sees value. The close is progressive commitment made explicit -- not a surprise, and never a manipulation when no value exists.
