# Prioritization Using the Sales Funnel
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on prioritization using the sales funnel.*

## Executive summary

The up and down sales revenue trend is not an embedded part of the sales process. Sellers who use the Buyer Engagement Forecast to prioritize their opportunities, and consistently allocate ample time, are more consistent revenue generators. When sellers focus on closing and prospecting, their sales Buyer Engagement Forecast remain full as opportunities work their way through the buyer engagement process. Prioritization Using the Sales Buyer Engagement Forecast Proper use of the sales Buyer Engagement Forecast assists sellers in spending their time wisely. Sellers who prioritize their activities in a systematic style are ensuring they have a regular stream of sales opportunities and revenue.

## Bottom line

The up and down sales revenue trend is not an embedded part of the sales process.
