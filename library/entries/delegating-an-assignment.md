# Delegating an Assignment
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on delegating an assignment.*

## Executive summary

New sales leaders are often very hesitant to delegate tasks to sellers. These leaders do not take the appropriate amount of time to become knowledgeable regarding the strengths, weaknesses, and skills of the sellers. Delegating tasks to appropriate sellers will free up time for the sales leader, improve morale, and build valuable skills and experience for the seller, ultimately making them greater assets to the company. By delegating the appropriate tasks, sales leaders are letting their sellers know they are trusted and valued. The sellers will feel as if they have been given an opportunity to show their capacity for quality work. The feedback provided to them will improve their skills and make them more valuable to the company.

## Bottom line

New sales leaders are often very hesitant to delegate tasks to sellers.
