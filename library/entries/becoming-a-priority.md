# Becoming a Priority
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on becoming a priority.*

## Executive summary

Sellers that do not put ample time into researching and planning can only hope for the best. Sellers that are dedicated to creating specific and specialized solutions that are based on the buyer's current priorities have a legitimate chance of doing business. These sellers can effectively relate a valid business reason to the buyer's current situation. When sellers establish themselves as a top priority, buyers will be more inclined to meet with them and be further engaged. The sellers's solutions will automatically be more buyer-centric, based on the buyer's priorities, receive more attention, and meet the mark.

## Bottom line

Sellers that do not put ample time into researching and planning can only hope for the best.
