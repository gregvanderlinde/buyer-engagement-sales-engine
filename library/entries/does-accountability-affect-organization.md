# Does Accountability Affect an Organization?
*Research on sales accountability systems and results.*

## Executive summary

Sales leaders must understand that only 10% of sellers take action when they hear an idea. In order to create a strong culture of accountability, sales leaders must conduct structured review meetings. More than 90% of sellers feel accountable for their actions when they participate in structured review meetings.

## Bottom line

Sales leaders must meet with staff regularly, gain action commitments from their team, and review actions and results. This is the key to creating a culture of accountability.
