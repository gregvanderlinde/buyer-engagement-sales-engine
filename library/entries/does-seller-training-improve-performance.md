# Does Investing in Seller Training Improve Performance?
*Research linking training investment to sales results.*

## Executive summary

While there is a direct correlation between the investment in seller training and sales results, companies are continuing to decrease their training investment by approximately 5% year over year. Additionally, the number of companies that offer no form of training is on the rise, with more than 5% of companies currently offering no form training to their sellers. There is a direct correlation between sales results and a company's investments in sales training for sellers.

## Bottom line

Continually investing in sellers training increases both departmental and sellers performance by over 10%.
