# Principles of Negotiation
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on principles of negotiation.*

## Executive summary

Negotiating is part of selling. Most sellers think it only happens at end of a deal during the close. However, sellers must be prepared to negotiate with buyers throughout the entire buyer engagement process. Therefore, sellers should have a well-prepared negotiation plan and understand how to use their plan to effectively negotiate the deal they envision closing. To eliminate last minute closing negotiations, sellers must realize that effective negotiation must take place throughout the sales process, thereby creating alignment and avoiding large concession negotiation during the close.

## Bottom line

Negotiating is part of selling.
