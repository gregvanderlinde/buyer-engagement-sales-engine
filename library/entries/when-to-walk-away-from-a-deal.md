# When to Walk Away from a Deal
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on when to walk away from a deal.*

## Executive summary

Walk away from the deal. Sellers often forget that this is an option. Sellers often spend too much time on sales that have no chance of closing. If sellers are having trouble getting a potential buyer to engage, align or commit, they should reconsider the opportunity. Sellers cannot forget about other prospects, and need to focus their time and resources on buyers who are actively engaged in the sale. Know when to walk away, know when to run. When buyers do not actively engage and commit, sellers lose time that could be better used working with other opportunities that are revenue generating. Sellers that spend too much time and resources on bad or dead leads severely reduce their ability to close deals.

## Bottom line

Walk away from the deal.
