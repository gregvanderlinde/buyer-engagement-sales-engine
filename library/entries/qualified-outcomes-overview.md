# Qualified Outcomes -- Overview
*The Numbers x The Win: a repeatable way to qualify and advance complex deals.*

## Executive summary

Qualified Outcomes is the method layer that turns buyer conversations into a clear match between what the buyer is trying to achieve and what only you can deliver. It is built on principles, a sequence, and a small set of tools -- not on pitch volume. The scoreboard is always The Numbers (business result) times The Win (personal), kept together as Qualified Outcomes.

## Bottom line

Institutionalize a consistent way to engage: match Unique Capabilities to prioritized work, quantify business and personal outcomes, bring Key Stakeholders in, and prove it with a Prove-to-Me Plan. Multiplication, not addition -- a zero in any critical factor means the deal is not ready.
