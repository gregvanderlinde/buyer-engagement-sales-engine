# Sales Leaders Must Be Motivators
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on sales leaders must be motivators.*

## Executive summary

Motivation plays a key role in the success of a sales department. Sales leaders who are able to find the source of motivation for their sellers will achieve outstanding results. While there are many motivational methods to consider, sales leaders must choose the methods that will draw out the desire of each individual seller to be successful. Sales leaders have to overcome seller tendencies to become complacent by creating innovative and challenging methods to motivate their sales team in order for them to operate consistently at an optimal level, ensuring desired results are being achieved.

## Bottom line

Motivation plays a key role in the success of a sales department.
