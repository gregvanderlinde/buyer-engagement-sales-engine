# Sales Leaders Must Enforce Prospecting
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on sales leaders must enforce prospecting.*

## Executive summary

Prospecting drives sales. Successful sales organizations embed prospecting in their sales culture and make it an integral part of their sales process. Sales leaders must ensure that their sellers prospect effectively and often. Sales leaders that allow their sellers to avoid prospecting quickly see sales opportunities diminish. Successful sales organizations have sales leaders that enforce prospecting, and therefore have a steady flow of opportunities in the sales Buyer Engagement Forecast. Sales leaders who consistently educate, enforce, monitor, measure, and compensate prospecting efforts will eliminate the roller-coaster sales Buyer Engagement Forecast effect, and yield higher revenue results, as there are more opportunities in the sales Buyer Engagement Forecast to consider.

## Bottom line

Prospecting drives sales.
