# Buyers Commitment to Action
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on buyers commitment to action.*

## Executive summary

Without commitment there cannot be a sale. Sellers must build trust with their potential buyers by offering incremental, realistic, and specific forms of commitment. Providing commitment advances sales, builds momentum, and engages potential buyers during each phase of the buyer engagement process. Buyer's Commitment to Action As sellers begin to understand the value of getting commitment from their buyers, and work on enhancing these skills, they will be able to easily assess whether the buyer is engaged or not, thereby moving the sale forward quicker. This will eliminate wasted time on buyers that are not engaged or committed, and free sellers up to focus on their ideal buyers.

## Bottom line

Without commitment there cannot be a sale.
