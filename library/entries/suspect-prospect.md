# Suspect Prospect
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on suspect prospect.*

## Executive summary

Before a company goes off and spends a bundle on some form of sales training they should focus on the skills they require so that they can execute on what they are buying.

## Bottom line

Master the core selling skills before you flood the market with activity -- skill turns suspects into real prospects.

## In this entry

- Suspect/Prospect
- Are they using similar offerings from the competition
- What is their process for buying
