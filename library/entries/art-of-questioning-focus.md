# The Art of Questioning - Questioning Focus
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on the art of questioning - questioning focus.*

## Executive summary

Sellers need to learn to focus their questioning methods on closing the gap between a buyer's current and ideal solution. Sellers that effectively focus on the buyer's perception of value regarding this situation will bridge the gap and close deals consistently. Sellers that can show buyers the difference between their current situation and the sales organization's product will be able to distance themselves from competition and create an incremental need that buyers want to follow.

## Bottom line

Sellers need to learn to focus their questioning methods on closing the gap between a buyer's current and ideal solution.
