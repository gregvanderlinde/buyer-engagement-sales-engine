# Pre-Call Planning
*Every interaction starts before you dial -- purpose, agenda, and desired actions.*

## Executive summary

Pre-call planning prepares you for encounters with buyers. It organizes the questions that create quality dialogue, builds a clear view of their situation, and establishes credibility so you can move past initial contact into the Buyer Engagement Process. Plan before every interaction, regardless of where you are in the deal.

## Bottom line

The sales process does not begin with the call. It begins when you actively research and prepare. Careful planning creates a strategic path, improves alignment, and saves time on weak opportunities.
