# Creating a Contagious Sales Culture
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on creating a contagious sales culture.*

## Executive summary

Having a sales culture is vital. Sales leaders who consistently redefine and communicate their vision to their organization provide their sales teams with a holistic, meaningful, and inspiring view of where the organization is currently, and where it is going in the future. As sales leaders create a vision that drives a culture, it enables the sales team to follow their process. In turn, this reduces costs and increases the effectiveness of sales Buyer Engagement Forecast.
