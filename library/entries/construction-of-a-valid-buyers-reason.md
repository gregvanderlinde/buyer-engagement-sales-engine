# Construction of a Valid Buyers Reason
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on construction of a valid buyers reason.*

## Executive summary

Sellers must have an objective for every action they take. Sellers that act without planning will not get far with buyers. Sellers must provide buyers with legitimate reasons to do business. These reasons should be based on what the sellers can do for a buyer to help them realize their ideal solution. Construction of a Valid Buyer's Reason If sellers cannot offer buyers a valid reason to meet within the first few minutes of initial contact, sellers will be hard pressed to re-establish contact and book future meetings to discuss a deal.

## Bottom line

Sellers must have an objective for every action they take.
