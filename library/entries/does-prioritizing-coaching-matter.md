# Does Prioritizing Coaching Make a Difference?
*Research on coaching priority and team performance.*

## Executive summary

More than one-third of companies believe they need to improve their ability to determine which sellers require coaching. Sellers, with the right coaching, can improve their performance by 5-10%. Sales leaders that prioritize their sellers and coach early on in the sales process are twice as effective at helping sellers develop their skills and provide value to their buyers. Coaching should be the number one priority for sales leaders. It is the number one tool to improve performance.

## Bottom line

Effectively prioritizing seller coaching is a cost effective method that improves performance, reduces turnover, and provides value to buyers.
