# What Is the Impact of Lead Nurturing?
*Research on nurture programs and conversion.*

## Executive summary

More than fifty percent of companies believe they need to improve their ability to nurture leads where buyers have displayed interest, but do not have time to properly evaluate the offering. Organizations need to support their sellers by nurturing leads until buyers are ready to make a purchase. Top companies successfully nurture leads, and understand the following.

## Bottom line

Top organizations effectively nurture leads, maintain buyer relationships, and pass leads to sellers when they are ready to make a purchase, therefore resulting in a better lead conversion rate.
