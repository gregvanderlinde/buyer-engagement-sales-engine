# The Buyer Engagement Approach
*Stop talking and forget about selling first -- partner from the buyer's point of view.*

## Executive summary

Stop leading with your agenda. The Buyer Engagement Approach creates a partnership: you and the
buyer work together, not with separate agendas, to move the sale forward. As you practice it, you
leave the self-centered, seller-centric habit behind. Buyers see their needs being addressed, and
credibility follows.

## Bottom line

Buyer need comes first -- your product knowledge second. Seek to understand before being understood.
Connect what you offer to their needs explicitly; never assume they will draw the line for you. That
is how you replace pitch-and-prove with a joint path to Qualified Outcomes.
