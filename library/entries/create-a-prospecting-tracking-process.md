# Create a Prospecting Tracking Process
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on create a prospecting tracking process.*

## Executive summary

Adhering to processes creates consistent results. Sales leaders who regularly track the prospecting efforts of their sellers see better, more consistent results. Making prospecting a company objective, and rewarding the efforts of sellers who prospect effectively on a consistent basis, will embed prospecting into the culture of the sales organization. Sales leaders cannot expect their sellers to prospect just because it's good for them and a best practice. They must create an environment that is conducive to driving the right behavior,including appropriate compensation components.

## Bottom line

Adhering to processes creates consistent results.
