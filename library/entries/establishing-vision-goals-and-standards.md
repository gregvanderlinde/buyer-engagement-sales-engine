# Establishing Vision Goals and Standards
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on establishing vision goals and standards.*

## Executive summary

Many new sales leaders fail to establish vision, goals, and standards before assuming their new role. Neglecting to do so puts these leaders at a significant disadvantage as compared to those who do. Setting goals and managing to an agenda essentially provides new sales leaders with a road map to success. Establishing Vision, Goals, and Standards for New Sales Leaders Without managing to a goal-directed agenda, both sales leaders and sellers can easily become bogged down with nit-picky tasks that have no relevance to their performance goals. Constantly reviewing goals and setting daily tasks based on these will ensure that they are targeting their efforts in the most effective manner possible.

## Bottom line

Many new sales leaders fail to establish vision, goals, and standards before assuming their new role.
