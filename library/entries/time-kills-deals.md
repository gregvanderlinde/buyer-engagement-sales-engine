# Time Kills Deals
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on time kills deals.*

## Executive summary

Delay is the silent killer of pursuits. When a buyer asks you to "call back in two weeks," almost anything can happen: the contact changes roles, advisors inject doubt, purchasing opens a competitive bid, or a competitor floods the account. Sellers who leave meetings without a next meeting scheduled, and who let built-in process delays drag, give time room to kill the deal.

## Bottom line

Never leave a meeting without the next meeting on the calendar, and strip every avoidable delay out of your sales process.

- What can happen while you wait
- How to protect selling time and force next steps
