# Does Differentiation Make a Difference?
*Research on clear differentiation versus commodity selling.*

## Executive summary

Year over year, sellers differentiation abilities continue to be one of the top three skill gaps. Over onethird of companies believe that this is a key performance issue. Effective differentiation skills are vital as buyers are less responsive to feature/benefit selling, and are more interested in a solution-based consultative sales approach.

## Bottom line

The ability to effectively differentiate and understand buyer needs allows sellers to take on a consultative role during the sales process, thereby improving their ability to secure deals.
