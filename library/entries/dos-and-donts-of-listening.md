# The Dos and Don'ts of Listening
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on the dos and don'ts of listening.*

## Executive summary

The act of listening respectfully includes exhibiting focus, patience, and both verbal and nonverbal actions. Many sales leaders have unconscious habits which make sellers feel ignored or anxious. Sales leaders must become aware of the do's and don'ts of listening in order to learn to behave in a way which will make sellers feel more comfortable coming forward with feedback and concerns. The Do's and Don'ts of Listening Sales leaders who can master the skills of both verbal and non-verbal communication with their sellers will have the information and ability required to lead far more effectively. This will ultimately result in improved morale and results. By making small, conscious changes to improve these skills, sellers will feel more comfortable and be willing to share valuable information with their leader in the future.

## Bottom line

The act of listening respectfully includes exhibiting focus, patience, and both verbal and nonverbal actions.
