# Do Incentive Plans Affect Seller Behavior?
*Research on how incentive design shapes selling behavior.*

## Executive summary

Commission plans that offer high upside potential increase seller retention and satisfaction, but do not increase performance. A commission plan that is aligned to seller behavior is crucial in driving sales results. Only two-thirds of companies believe that their commission plan incents the desired activities and behaviors from their sellers. Benefits of an Aligned Commission Plan Effective incentive plans drive sales behavior.

## Bottom line

Compensation plans with a high upside for sellers need to be aligned with sales activities and drive the right behavior to achieve desired business results.
