# Does Your Company Benefit from Seller Training?
*Research on sales skill training investment and forecast quality.*

## Executive summary

More than fifty percent of companies believe they need to improve their current sales skill training. The companies that invest the least in training acknowledged a need to improve more than twice as much as companies that adequately invest in sales skill training. Effective sales training impacts a seller's ability to work through the sales process and accurately forecast opportunities. Organizations must regularly provide sales skill training in order to see consistent performance improvements.

## Bottom line

In an environment where product features and branding are no longer enough to close deals, sales skills are the primary means to improve performance, differentiate, and provide value to buyers.
