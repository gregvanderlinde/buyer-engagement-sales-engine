# Networking Externally
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on networking externally.*

## Executive summary

Networking does not always come naturally or comfortably to all sales people. It is not uncommon for sellers and sales leaders to be shy. These people often feel overwhelmed and intimidated when faced with loud extroverts and cliques while networking. However, networking is a mandatory function for sales people, and must be a priority. Networking allows sales leaders to:
- Expand their reach into the competitive world of sales.
- Make the right contacts
- Prospect with improved results
- Open doors for their company's product or service As sales leaders and sellers embrace networking as a form of expanding their influence and prospecting abilities, they must actively work on their networking abilities and put on a persona that is conducive to making valuable contacts.

## Bottom line

Networking does not always come naturally or comfortably to all sales people.
