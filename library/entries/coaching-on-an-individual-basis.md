# Coaching on an Individual Basis
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on coaching on an individual basis.*

## Executive summary

No two sellers are the same. Sales leaders must consider the difference experience levels, coaching styles, and sales knowledge sellers possess. Always coach each sellers on an individual basis to ensure maximum benefit and outcome. Whether the sellers are experienced, new, or consistently missing quota, sales leaders must focus on a consistent sales process as the basis for their coaching sessions and as the one constant. Coach on an Individual Basis Sales leaders will see greater results by to ensure each sellers receives the attention required to improve their ability to sell. Focusing on consistent sales process as common ground will eliminate other variables and ensure sellers understand and follow the sales process diligently.

## Bottom line

No two sellers are the same.
