# Benefits of a Forecasting Process
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on benefits of a forecasting process.*

## Executive summary

Effective forecasting is not an easy task. It is strongly connected to the accuracy of the information that sellers provide to their sales leaders about the status of their current open opportunities. Sales leaders then impart this information to their contemporaries within the company to help them make key decisions. To increase the accuracy of the information retrieved from sellers, sales leaders need to institute a forecasting process that tracks open opportunities in an efficient manner. Forecasting must be based on the principles of an effective sales process which includes the necessary elements and has a specific set of expectations and rules for sellers to follow. When combined with diligent forecasting management from the sales leader, it will provide a more accurate forecast so that sales leaders are equipped to make more efficient decisions.

## Bottom line

A defined forecasting process replaces vague updates with facts so leaders can make sound business decisions.
