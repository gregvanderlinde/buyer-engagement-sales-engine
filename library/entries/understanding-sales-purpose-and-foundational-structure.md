# Understanding Sales Purpose and Foundational Structure
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on understanding sales purpose and foundational structure.*

## Executive summary

Every interaction with a buyer has a common purpose and structure. Even though no two sales processes are the same, they share fundamental steps and sales engagement methods. These set the stage for a seller's buyer engagement process. No matter what organization sellers are part of, there is a common purpose and direction to their sales calls. While each sellers has a different approach, following these common practices will guide sellers through the sales process no matter what their level of experience.

## Bottom line

Every interaction with a buyer has a common purpose and structure.
