# Differentiating Sellers from the Competition
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on differentiating sellers from the competition.*

## Executive summary

Markets are fierce and competition is intense. In order to survive, companies must introduce a unique product to their buyers. To be able do so, sellers have to identity their competition, research what their competitors are offering, and create a competitive strategy based on this information. Differentiating sellers from the Competition While it is important to understand competitors, the best way to differentiate is by demonstrating value and proving to buyers how that offering will satisfy key business issues.

## Bottom line

Markets are fierce and competition is intense.
