# How Does New Lead Generation Affect Selling?
*Research on new-lead programs and seller time use.*

## Executive summary

Approximately two-thirds of companies believe they need to improve their ability to generate new leads. More than one third of executives identified improving lead generation programs as their key initiative for 2010.

## Bottom line

Industry leaders that drive lead generation activities and effectively evaluate their sellers are more successful at lead generation, and will turn more opportunities into deals that close.
