# Does Assessment Testing Affect Hire Quality?
*Research on assessment testing in sales hiring.*

## Executive summary

Using assessment testing as part of a company's hiring process improves the quality of individuals hired. Over 90% of companies who use assessment testing believe it has a positive impact on hiring. While assessment testing has a positive impact on hiring, still less than half of companies use it as part of their hiring process.

## Bottom line

Using assessment testing during the hiring process has a significant impact on a company's ability to hire the correct individual, reduces costs, and improves seller performance.
