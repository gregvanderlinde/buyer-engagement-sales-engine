# Does the Seller-Buyer Relationship Make a Difference?
*Research on relationship quality and win rates.*

## Executive summary

About one-third of sellers are viewed by their buyers as consultative partners. About one-third of selling organizations say they need to improve buyer loyalty. Only 25-50% of buyers claim loyalty to the company and seller they work with today -- both a risk and an opening for sales leaders.

## Bottom line

A deep, loyal relationship with buyers is central to meeting their needs, achieving quota, and continuing to grow revenue from existing accounts.
