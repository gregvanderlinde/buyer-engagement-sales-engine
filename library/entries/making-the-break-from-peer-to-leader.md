# Making the Break from Peer to Leader
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on making the break from peer to leader.*

## Executive summary

Sales leaders who have been promoted from within a company are commonly concerned about how their new role will impact their relationships with current friends and co-workers. A good leader knows the importance of separating friendship and work. New sales leaders need to embrace their role as a leader and make conscious, positive steps to separate themselves from their previous peers. This will enable them to lead from a position of authority and reduce any resistance.
