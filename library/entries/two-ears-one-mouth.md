# Two Ears One Mouth
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on two ears one mouth.*

## Executive summary

Selling is the art of asking questions, listening openly and intentionally, and gaining information; not giving it, or as a learned associate of mine says "Be a seller, not a teller".

## Bottom line

Listen more than you talk; buyers reveal what matters when you keep your mouth closed and your ears open.
