# Do CRMs Improve Selling Time and Performance?
*Research on CRM use, selling time, and quota attainment.*

## Executive summary

Sales leaders expect sellers to spend at least 50% of their time performing selling activities. The amount of seller time spent on selling has continued to decline in recent years. In fact, sellers currently only spend approximately one-third of their time selling (15 hours of a 40 hour work week). This gap in time spent selling has created concern, and must be addressed by sales leaders.

## Bottom line

Sales leaders must continually work with sellers to increase selling time. CRM implementation helps sellers balance their time effectively and increase selling time, which is correlated to increased quota achievement.
