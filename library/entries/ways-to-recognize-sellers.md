# Ways to Recognize Sellers
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on ways to recognize sellers.*

## Executive summary

Everyone wants to feel appreciated and be recognized for a job well done. There are various mechanisms for rewarding performance depending on the task, time of year, and the individual. Recognition helps to: create job satisfaction, reinforce desired performance and behaviors, build self-esteem, and shape a company's culture that attracts and retains high quality sales staff. The keys to effective recognition are: sincerity, timeliness, specificity, personalization, and appropriateness. Ways to Recognize sellers When used to stimulate productivity by playing on competitiveness, highly visible tactical/symbolic rewards are often just as motivational as tangible/personal rewards. Each sellers is motivated by different triggers. Therefore, incentive programs are most useful when they utilize a combination of different types of rewards.

## Bottom line

Everyone wants to feel appreciated and be recognized for a job well done.
