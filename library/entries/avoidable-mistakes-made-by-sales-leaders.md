# Avoidable Mistakes Made by Sales Leaders
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on avoidable mistakes made by sales leaders.*

## Executive summary

Sellers must be managed effectively. Sales leaders have to understand their sellers' capabilities in order to place them in an ideal sales environment where they can have a positive impact on the company and sales results. Sales leaders who can successfully identify their sellers capabilities will be able to utilize their talents in the proper context. This will create a more efficient sales team while working within the context of their sales skills. Sales leaders cannot fall into the trap of promoting top sellers based on their sales numbers. Nor can they fall into the trap of assigning sellers to accounts that are above their skill level. When accounts are poorly assigned, sales leaders risk facing the repercussions of having an ineffective sales team that struggles, fails to reach their potential, and does not achieve their sales goals.

## Bottom line

Sellers must be managed effectively.
