# Identify the Outcome of Making Contact
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on identify the outcome of making contact.*

## Executive summary

Sellers often get face time with buyers (either current or prospective) due to sheer persistence, rather than legitimately compelling them to engage in a discovery process. Sellers that always use valid, buyer-centric reasons to initiate contact evoke far more real engagement. True buyer engagement creates a stimulating environment where conversation occurs freely, and monosyllabic responses are eliminated. Therefore, sellers can elicit buyer pains with far less difficulty. Sellers need to be prepared for all of the different types of buying situations they find themselves in, and use the predefined strategies to deal with buyers accordingly.

## Bottom line
