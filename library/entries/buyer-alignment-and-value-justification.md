# Buyer Alignment and Value Justification
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on buyer alignment and value justification.*

## Executive summary

Solutions must be justified. Sellers who justify the value of a solution based on the return of the buyer's investment, revenue growth, and cost savings make their solution more appealing. Justifying value provides buyers with a legitimate reason to take action and engage in the deal. Sellers who seek to discover the standard by which buyers measure value during the sales process will know how to sell to buyers more effectively, and as a result, will close more often.

## Bottom line

Solutions must be justified.
