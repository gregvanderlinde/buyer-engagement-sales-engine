# Engage -- Not Pitch and Prove
*Break the habit of pitching a claim and hoping they believe it -- engage from their side of the table.*

## Executive summary

Sales are about buyers. Too often sellers overlook the job: serve the buyer effectively. Break the
habit of pitching and proving. Do your research, run the Buyer Engagement Process as the basis of
every dealing, and you will be more effective. Size does not matter -- if you keep selling without
their perspective, competitors who engage will win the business.

## Bottom line

Pitch-and-prove has a wildly variable hit rate and hides whether the buyer is serious or just
shopping for leverage. Adopt a win/win mentality: understand situation, motivators, influencers, and
timelines; help them form a vision of the ideal solution; then connect your offer to that vision on
purpose.
