# How to Give Effective Feedback
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on how to give effective feedback.*

## Executive summary

Many new sales leaders undermine the importance of feedback as a skill. Regular, effective feedback drives a change in seller behavior that improves their performance and contributes to attaining overall targets. The main goal of feedback is to drive a change in behavior which will impact results. The sales leader cannot change the behavior of the seller - only they can do this. By giving effective feedback, the leader can help the sellers recognize the nature and triggers of the problem, and agree upon next steps which will ideally improve performance.

## Bottom line

Many new sales leaders undermine the importance of feedback as a skill.
