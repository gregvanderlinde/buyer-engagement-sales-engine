# Valuable Feedback in Real Time for Sales Leaders
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on valuable feedback in real time for sales leaders.*

## Executive summary

A large amount of vital information and feedback is often lost from meetings and presentations. Quick and organized sessions are the key to capturing unspoken thoughts that sellers are often hesitant to voice during meetings and training sessions. These valuable after-meeting sessions are referred to by the US military as "hotwash" sessions. Taking the time to hold these short feedback meetings will ensure that points are clear, actions are taken, and that future meetings are run effectively. A hotwash session is not intended to make work for the team. It is simply intended to make them aware. It is an opportunity for the sales leader to get much needed honest and open feedback from sellers. Take this information and use it to make incremental changes going forward.
