# Situation Analysis
*Assess each opportunity so you chase fit -- and walk away from what is not.*

## Executive summary

Every sales situation is unique. Situation analysis helps you determine which funnel opportunities have potential to close and which buyers are not a fit. Done well, it aligns you with real buyers, improves close odds, and protects time and resources from weak pursuits.

## Bottom line

Analyze unknowns in the buyer's specific situation -- not facts you should already know from pre-call planning. Continuous assessment lowers the risk of following the wrong opportunity.
