# Prospecting with Qualified Outcomes
*Use the same match factors to open new conversations -- not a separate pitch track.*

## Executive summary

Prospecting with Qualified Outcomes means opening with Business Objectives and prioritized work, then earning a Unique Capability Match through questions -- the same spine as later-stage work, earlier and lighter. You are hunting for a real match factor set, not for permission to demo.

## Bottom line

Lead with their objectives and initiatives. Deepen into concerns and current approach. Only then offer Unique Capabilities and a path to quantify outcomes. Qualify or fail fast.
