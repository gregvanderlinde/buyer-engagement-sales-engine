# Minimum Engagement Step
*The floor -- the least the buyer must do for the pursuit to be real.*

## Executive summary

Every meeting needs a floor as well as a ceiling. The Minimum Engagement Step is the least the buyer must do after this conversation for the pursuit to stay real, and it is always an action the buyer takes. "Let's meet again" is not a step on its own: a next meeting counts only when the buyer takes a role in it. When a buyer will not reach the floor, you find out why, re-read their Gap State and Fit Six, and slow down or walk away if a factor is zero.

## Bottom line

Decide before every meeting the least the buyer must do for this pursuit to stay real -- and when they will not do it, find out why instead of booking another meeting that moves nothing.
