# Stimulating Buyer Interest Through Prospecting
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on stimulating buyer interest through prospecting.*

## Executive summary

Sellers who consistently prospect generate interest and create awareness for their products. Sales opportunities are created on a regular and consistent basis when prospecting is done effectively. This integral part of a seller's job creates a sales Buyer Engagement Forecast with opportunities that have a legitimate interest in doing business. Ambitious sellers who focus their prospecting on piquing the interest of and developing relationships with buyers will generate more opportunities to close and be in a position to generate more revenue.

## Bottom line

Sellers who consistently prospect generate interest and create awareness for their products.
