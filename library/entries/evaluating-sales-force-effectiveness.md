# Evaluating Sales Force Effectiveness
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on evaluating sales force effectiveness.*

## Executive summary

Sales leaders must avoid the trap of "pay more or change the compensation plan" to motivate sellers and increase sales. There are many issues which can affect sellers effectiveness depending on the company and industry. Compensation plans which are relatively simple, easy to understand, and clearly link seller success to company success, will be successful in driving seller performance. There is no silver bullet for motivating and compensating sellers. Any changes made to a compensation program must be done with careful consideration, due diligence, and from a holistic perspective.

## Bottom line

Sales leaders must avoid the trap of "pay more or change the compensation plan" to motivate sellers and increase sales.
