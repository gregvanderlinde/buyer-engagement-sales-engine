# Managing Upper Management
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on managing upper management.*

## Executive summary

Most new sales leaders do not even think about what it means to manage their manager. This inevitably hurts their performance in the eyes of upper management. Maintaining open communication, aligning agreed upon targets, and managing expectations will ensure that both parties measure success the same way, ensuring that the efforts of the sales leader are not lost. It is just as important for a sales leader to manage their manager as it is to manage their sellers. This is achieved with frequent, concise, upward communication, ensuring the goals of both managers are aligned, and setting and exceeding expectations.

## Bottom line

Most new sales leaders do not even think about what it means to manage their manager.
