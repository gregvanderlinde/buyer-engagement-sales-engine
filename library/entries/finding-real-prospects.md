# Finding Real Prospects
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on finding real prospects.*

## Executive summary

Real prospects are not found with marketing efforts. Prospects are discovered when sellers dedicate themselves to a prospecting process that focuses on buyers that are well suited for the product they are selling. This eliminates the need to count on leads from marketing that may not be properly nurtured to generate sales. Sellers who commit themselves to an effective prospecting process do not have to rely on marketing for leads and are able to concentrate on quality legitimate opportunities in their Buyer Engagement Forecast by focusing on the buyer's needs as a means to engage them in the sales process.

## Bottom line

Real prospects are not found with marketing efforts.
