# Does Communicating Best Practices Matter?
*Research on sharing winning practices across the team.*

## Executive summary

Approximately half of all companies need to improve their ability to communicate best practices. More than 90% of top companies know the personality traits and characteristics of their top sellers, and communicate this to the rest of the organization. It is a constant challenge to keep sales knowledge current.

## Bottom line

Top companies enable their sellers to increase performance, reduce sales time, and achieve quota more often when best practices are effectively communicated throughout the organization.
