# CPRD Workshop Instructions for Leaders
*How to run a light, respectful group CPRD session so people see blends -- and laugh at their own foibles.*

## Executive summary

A CPRD workshop helps a team understand themselves and each other without boxing anyone in. You administer a short adjective self-check, score the four quadrants, make styles visible with colored sticky notes, then facilitate discovery of strengths, fears, and rapid recognition cues. Keep it fun and clear: no style is better than another; each has gifts and shortcomings. The workshop materials also point sellers toward how to approach buyers of each blend.

## Bottom line

Run CPRD as a group mirror -- visible, light, and practical -- so people flex toward difference instead of defending their default.

- How to administer and display the four-quadrant read.
- Facilitation questions that teach recognition in 30-60 seconds.
- What the workshop materials cover (self-assessment, cues, selling moves).
