# Does Product Training Make a Difference?
*Research on product knowledge training versus skill training balance.*

## Executive summary

Product training is often the focus for the majority of sellers training. Over one-third of companies still identify the need to improve product training. Product training must be continuous, focused, and delivered in regular actionable parts. Competition consistently drives change.

## Bottom line

Continuous product knowledge training reduces selling time, allows sellers to align their unique capabilities of their product to address buyer specific needs, and positions sellers in consultative sales roles with their buyers.
