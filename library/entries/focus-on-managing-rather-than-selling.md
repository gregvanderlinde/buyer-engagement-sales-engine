# Focus on Managing Rather then Selling
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on focus on managing rather then selling.*

## Executive summary

Sales leaders get caught up in tactical deals. They often spend too much time working with sellers on deals rather than focusing on leading their team. Effective sales leaders limit their time spent working on deals and ensure they spend sufficient time preparing sales initiatives, improving team performance, coaching and training, and hiring. While there is a time and place for sales leader involvement, they cannot lose sight of their primary objective as the leader of the team. Focus on Managing Rather than Selling While there is a time and place for their involvement with sellers and potential deals, sales leaders need to limit their time with sellers to ensure they are focused on creating a successful sales environment for the present and into the future.

## Bottom line

Sales leaders get caught up in tactical deals.
