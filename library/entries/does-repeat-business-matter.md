# Does Repeat Business from Existing Accounts Matter?
*Research on protecting and expanding the existing book.*

## Executive summary

60% of revenue comes from existing buyers. Companies need to have a continued focus on existing accounts to protect their book of business. Increased competitive pressure will see companies aggressively seeking new business. Renewals need to be a top priority.

## Bottom line

A company's ability to generate repeat business from existing buyers through effective communication, differentiation training, and deal reviews will help them maintain current accounts in this highly competitive environment.
