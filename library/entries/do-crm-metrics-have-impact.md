# Do Metrics from CRM Use Have an Impact?
*Research on CRM metrics discipline and performance lift.*

## Executive summary

Organizations that use a CRM and corresponding metrics are twice as likely to create and manage an effective buyer centric sales process. Over 40% of sales leaders do not have access to effective information metrics. Benefits of the Use of Metrics Access to metrics from CRM adoption is an important part of sales management. Performance indicators help sales leaders make more accurate and fact-based decisions.

## Bottom line

Top sales leaders consistently manage the use of CRMs to ensure they get effective metrics to make better and more informed decisions. CRM and metric usage helps manage the sales process, and improves seller and departmental results.
