# Pre Meeting Preparation
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on pre meeting preparation.*

## Executive summary

Pre-meeting preparation effectively sets the stage for a deal to transpire. Sellers must have a comprehensive understanding of a buyer's status, needs, and inefficiencies. They must have a plan to meet these needs before a meeting is set. Successful sellers always plan ahead. Pre-meetings will improve a seller's chance of engaging buyers and developing an ideal solution to meet their needs. Pre-Meeting Preparation: A Vital Stage in the Sales Process sellers that effectively plan their sales calls will have a better understanding of a buyer's status, needs, pain, and goals, and will be in a better position to properly develop an ideal solution resulting in more closed deals and increased revenue.

## Bottom line

Pre-meeting preparation effectively sets the stage for a deal to transpire.
