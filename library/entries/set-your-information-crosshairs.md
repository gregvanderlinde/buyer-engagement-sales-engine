# Set Your Information Crosshairs
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on set your information crosshairs.*

## Executive summary

Sellers must have an effective questioning process that uncovers the information they need to gain an understanding of their buyer's situation. A well-planned process helps sellers avoid questioning pitfalls and focus on asking questions that help them develop alignment and engage buyers in the sales process. Asking purposeful questions in the correct sequence will get sellers the information to create understanding and help them align and engage buyers, increasing their odds of closing the deal.
