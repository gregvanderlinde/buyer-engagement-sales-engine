# Differentiate It's Better to Stand out than to Blend In
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on differentiate it's better to stand out than to blend in.*

## Executive summary

Be uniquely different. Sellers that can offer buyers intangibles will garner attention. It is the subtle differences that create an advantage between competitors. Sellers that can effectively differentiate themselves from their competitors by focusing on the buyer's situation, and offering a unique solution, will be preferred. Differentiate - It Is Better to Stand out than to Blend In sellers must offer differentiated solutions. Failure to clearly create differentiation from their competitors will cause sellers to blend into the marketplace and become one of many, instead of a unique option for buyers.

## Bottom line

Be uniquely different.
