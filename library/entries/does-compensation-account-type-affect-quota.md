# Does Compensation and Account Type Affect Quota?
*Research on pay design, account mix, and quota hit rates.*

## Executive summary

Greater upside potential comes from increased variable compensation. Half of all sellers have a variable component that comprises up to 50% of their salary. The higher the sales target, the greater the corresponding need to sell into existing accounts. Sales leaders must understand the relationship between quota and compensation in relation to seller performance.

## Bottom line

Even in hunter roles with large quotas, there is a need to have existing accounts in order to achieve target. Failure to include existing accounts as part of quota results in a lack of and inconsistent revenue, reduced seller satisfaction, and higher seller churn.
