# Networking Internally
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on networking internally.*

## Executive summary

Everyone in the business world knows that networking is an essential part of their job. However, most people dread the task and do not see how failing to network restricts their performance. Internal networking makes work for sales leaders easier and faster while promoting rapid career growth. The sales department is not an island within a company. It relies on all parts of the organization to function. Internal networking is imperative to ensure that the department operates as a well-oiled machined.
