# Evaluating Compensation Plan Effectiveness
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on evaluating compensation plan effectiveness.*

## Executive summary

Desperate times call for desperate measures. Often times, that measure is a change in compensation plan, with the hope that sellers performance will improve. Unfortunately, this change usually results in little to no improvement. firmly believes that a sales compensation plan is only as good as the sellers that it inspires, and the level of alignment between seller targets, performance measures, and overall company strategies. No compensation plan is perfect, and there is no "one-size-fits-all" solution. Compensation effectiveness must be evaluated and adjusted without a knee-jerk reaction to ensure the correct methods are used.

## Bottom line

Desperate times call for desperate measures.
