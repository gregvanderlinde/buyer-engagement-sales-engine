# Does Sales Methodology Make a Difference?
*Research on adopting a shared sales methodology.*

## Executive summary

Approximately 90% of companies believe their sales methodology has a positive impact the performance of their sellers. One third of these companies believe that a sales methodology has a significant impact on their organization's overall effectiveness.

## Bottom line

Consistent use of effective sales methodologies positively impacts sales process execution, increases forecast accuracy and quota attainment, and helps organizations win more deals.
