# Relative Uniqueness
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on relative uniqueness.*

## Executive summary

Buyers must see the unique capabilities in the solution created for them. True uniqueness is rare due to the nature of today's competitive market. Therefore, sellers have to create a based on their product's features so that they can differentiate themselves from competitors and align themselves to the specifics of each buyer's situation. When there is little distinction between competitors' offerings, sellers must offer a that will differentiate them from the competition, or they will not make the impact required to win the deal and collect the revenue.

## Bottom line

Buyers must see the unique capabilities in the solution created for them.
