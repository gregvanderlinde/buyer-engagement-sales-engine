# Don't Give Without Getting
*Insist on buyer engagement -- every give earns a commitment in return.*

## Executive summary

Too many sellers hand over quotes, proposals, samples, testimonials, and discounts because the buyer
says they need them for a "responsible purchase process" -- and ask for nothing back. That creates a
lose/win environment. Real Buyer Engagement is give and take. Insist on a form of commitment in
return for the work you do to develop their ideal solution.

## Bottom line

If the buyer will not commit to an engagement action when you give, they are fishing -- not engaging.
Treat every request as a negotiation. A healthy rhythm is roughly balanced; when you are doing nearly
all the giving, stop and reassess before you dig deeper.
