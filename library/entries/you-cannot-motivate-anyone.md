# You Cannot Motivate Anyone
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on you cannot motivate anyone.*

## Executive summary

People cannot be motivated. This is true no matter how strong the sales leader or how great the company. Instead, focus should be put on finding out what motivates sellers and creating an environment which fosters these motivations. Self-motivated sellers require less time from their leader, consistently produce desirable results, and have less behavioral issues. A successful leader must therefore spend time developing an effective set of motivational tools and resources to support their sellers in their self-motivation efforts. As sales leaders move away from generic "One size fits all" type incentives, they will reap the rewards of having a team which is more self-motivated to achieve their personal, and thus business, goals.

## Bottom line

People cannot be motivated.
