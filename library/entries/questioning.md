# Questioning
*Ask the right mode at the right time: open, guided, then closed.*

## Executive summary

Effective questioning uncovers the information that drives both transactional and complex sales. Strong questions give you a clear view of the buyer's status and show what still must be collected to build momentum. Know three modes -- open, guided, and closed -- and use each for its purpose instead of running a mechanical survey.

## Bottom line

Questioning creates understanding. Focus on the buyer's specific situation, listen actively, and sequence open exploration, guided depth, and closed confirmation.

## In this entry

- Why effective questioning beats product-push surveys.
- Open questions that invite unexpected insight.
- Guided questions that drill and clarify.
- Closed questions that confirm, align, and lock understanding.
