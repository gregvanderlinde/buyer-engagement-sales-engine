# Focus on the Payoff
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on focus on the payoff.*

## Executive summary

It is the top sellers who will make up revenue shortcomings. Sales leaders must avoid the temptation to focus on B and C level sellers to improve their revenue numbers. Coaching that is focused on the team's leading sellers skills will yield more revenue with less coaching time. Sales leaders must focus on their best payoff: Top sellers. Focus time coaching and investing in top sellers and identifying B level sellers who demonstrate the potential to generate more revenue.

## Bottom line

It is the top sellers who will make up revenue shortcomings.
