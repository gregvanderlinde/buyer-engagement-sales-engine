# Features and Benefits
*Only useful when they are relevant to the buyer's needs and ideal solution.*

## Executive summary

Features and benefits create interest only when they align to what the buyer is trying to fix, avoid, or achieve. Feature dumping hopes the buyer will find value on their own. The Buyer Engagement Process does the opposite: question first, confirm needs, then present only the features and benefits the buyer has already said matter.

## Bottom line

A feature is a fact about what you sell. A benefit is the result the buyer gets when that fact meets a confirmed need. Value appears only after the buyer validates that the benefit is what they are looking for.

- A simple example of relevance over completeness.
