# Mutual Commitment
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on mutual commitment.*

## Executive summary

While is a requirement for the sales process, sellers must not commit to the sale if buyers are not willing to do the same. Commitment from sellers must align with what buyers are willing to put into the sales process. Commitment requires engagement, alignment, and action from both parties for a deal to be reached. When commitment is present from both sellers and buyers, a win/win sales environment is created. This enhances the focus of developing an ideal solution and increases the seller's chances of closing the deal.

## Bottom line

While is a requirement for the sales process, sellers must not commit to the sale if buyers are not willing to do the same.
