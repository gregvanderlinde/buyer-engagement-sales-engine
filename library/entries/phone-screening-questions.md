# Phone Screening Questions
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on phone screening questions.*

## Executive summary

Effective phone screening is an important part of the interview process. When done effectively, it respects everyone's time, and more importantly, it allows the interviewer to select and invest time in only the most suitable candidates.

## Bottom line

Phone screens exist to decide fit quickly -- keep them focused on whether a live interview is worth both parties' time.
