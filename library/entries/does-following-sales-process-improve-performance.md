# Does Following a Sales Process Improve Performance?
*Research on process adherence and quota outcomes.*

## Executive summary

More than half of all companies do not have a formal sales process in place. Lack of an effective sales process greatly reduces an organization's ability to maximize performance, achieve revenue, and forecast accurately. The top third of companies that rank themselves as satisfactory or greater in achieving sales targets believe that a sales process improves their sales effectiveness. Organizations benefit from having an effective sales process in place for their sellers to follow.

## Bottom line

Implementing a systematic sales process has a measureable impact on the sales performance of an organization.
