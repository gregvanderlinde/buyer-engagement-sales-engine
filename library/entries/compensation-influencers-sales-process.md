# Compensation Influencers - The Sales Process
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on compensation influencers - the sales process.*

## Executive summary

There is no single factor that can determine what an appropriate compensation structure is for a company. What is fair and reasonable is influenced by a large number of variables, many of which are outside the sellers's control. The sales process, brand awareness, and product maturity all influence compensation ratios. When properly constructed, compensation plans will provide sellers with security while promoting desirable behaviors and outcomes. Compensation Influencers : The Sales Process Compensation is driven in part by company and product maturity. Sales leaders will harvest the greatest results from a well thought-out compensation plan, and avoid incorrectly paying and motivating sellers.
