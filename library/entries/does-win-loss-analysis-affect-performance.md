# Does Win/Loss Analysis Affect Performance?
*Research on systematic win/loss review and improvement.*

## Executive summary

Approximately 60% of companies believe that they need to improve their regular usage of deal reviews. Still, only 20% of companies conduct regular win/loss analysis with an unbiased and focused process. Deal reviews uncover information about what should be done (or not done) to improve process, increase understanding of buyers, and ultimately drive results. Only one-third of all sellers receive an honest response from buyers as to why they lost a deal.

## Bottom line

Organizations that regularly conduct win/loss analysis will improve efficiencies, their sales process, current trends, and their understanding of buyer's decision making criteria.
