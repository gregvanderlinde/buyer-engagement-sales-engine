# Creating Opportunities Through Networking
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on creating opportunities through networking.*

## Executive summary

Networking allows sellers to create relationships that would never develop outside of regular business. Sellers who include networking as part of their regular prospecting efforts will benefit from the relationships they develop. These relationships will lead to new sales open opportunities with both existing and new buyers. Sellers who consistently maintain contact with buyers and industry people will create more channels of communication to prospect new sales opportunities, giving them the advantage over their competition.

## Bottom line

Networking allows sellers to create relationships that would never develop outside of regular business.
