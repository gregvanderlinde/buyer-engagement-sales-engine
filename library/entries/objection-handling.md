# Objection Handling
*Objections are normal. Diagnose the type, then re-align.*

## Executive summary

Objections show up throughout a deal -- on features, on closes, on claims, or as stalls and excuses. They usually signal missing alignment or unclear value, not a lost deal. Know the three categories (misunderstanding, drawback, condition), then follow a calm sequence: listen, rephrase, question, offer a solution, confirm -- so you can re-engage and move forward.

## Bottom line

An objection is information. Prepare for it, classify it, and work with the buyer to restore alignment instead of debating or freezing.
