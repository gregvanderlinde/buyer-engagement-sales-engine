# Profiling Sellers
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on profiling sellers.*

## Executive summary

Sales leaders are responsible for the performance of the sales team. Inspiring the performance of sellers means providing incentives to encourage self-motivation. The value of the incentives provided depends entirely on the seller. If the incentive has no relevance to them, then it is essentially worthless. This is why it is imperative for sales managers to ascertain their staff's ambitions in order to know how to motivate them and create a highperforming team. Profiling sellers Sales leaders will only be able to effectively motivate sellers once they get to know their needs and goals by developing a relationship with them. Keeping a profile will help them to remember details about the sellers and will help to develop timelines for achievement. The best incentive program is a customized one.

## Bottom line

Sales leaders are responsible for the performance of the sales team.
