# Does Effective Onboarding Make a Difference?
*Research on formal onboarding and time-to-productivity.*

## Executive summary

Approximately one-quarter of sellers in an organization are new to their position. On-boarding is linked to business profitability, and new employees have a direct impact on productivity and profitability.

## Bottom line

Hiring is an expensive proposition. Top companies must invest in formalized on-boarding programs to cement hiring, build seller engagement, improve time management, and improve results.
