# Do Onboarding Practices Matter?
*Research on formal onboarding plus training and ramp speed.*

## Executive summary

Two thirds of top organizations follow up onboarding with effective training programs to engage sellers. Companies need to hire and train sellers who have the ability to sell their products, adapt to change and meet buyer expectations.

## Bottom line

Onboarding occurs when sales leader expectations are concurrent with onboarding initiatives, and training is aligned with current business goals and objectives.
