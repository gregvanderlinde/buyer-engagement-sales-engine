# The Sales Formula for Success
*A buyer-centric checklist: business issue, Gap, stakeholders, plan, value, differentiated solution, ideal solution.*

## Executive summary

Successful selling revolves around meeting buyer needs through a clear process. The sales formula multiplies the factors that must be present: a real business issue, a Gap of need, key stakeholders, a plan, value (business and personal), a unique differentiated solution, and an ideal solution that works for both sides. Miss a factor and the deal weakens; work them deliberately and relationships and closes follow.

## Bottom line

Treat the formula as a discipline, not a slogan. When each factor is present and confirmed with the buyer, you are positioned to improve their current situation and earn commitment.
