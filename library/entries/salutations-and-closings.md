# Salutations & Closings
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on salutations & closings.*

## Executive summary

Salutations and closings set the tone of written outreach. Choose forms that match what you know about the reader's name and gender, and keep closings consistent with the formality of the relationship.

## Bottom line

Match salutation and closing to the relationship and gender cues you actually know -- formality signals respect.
