# Combination Compensation Plans
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on combination compensation plans.*

## Executive summary

provide leaders with maximum flexibility. When designed properly they can incent and reward a number of key actions and outcomes. However, the effectiveness of these plans decrease as more metrics and targets are added and focus is lost. As sales leaders strive to incent sellers and drive the right types of behaviors, the compensation plans they choose need to be carefully considered and effectively implemented and managed. However, poor implementation can prevent any changes in behavior and drive compensation costs up.

## Bottom line

provide leaders with maximum flexibility.
