# Root Cause Questions
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on root cause questions.*

## Executive summary

uncover the basic issues that are preventing buyers from committing to the next steps in the sales process. Sellers must be prepared to ask these questions to dig deep and uncover any reservations buyers may have when they show hesitation about moving forward with the deal. Sellers who understand when to ask to query a potential buyer's objections on a deeper level will be able to align and engage buyers more often and have a higher closing rate.
