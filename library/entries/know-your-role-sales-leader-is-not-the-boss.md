# Know Your Role Within the Company- A Sales Leader is Not the Boss
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on know your role within the company- a sales leader is not the boss.*

## Executive summary

Today's sales organizations focus on a team first approach. Sales leaders no longer make decisions unilaterally; rather, decisions are made in conjunction with the management team. Sales leaders must break free from the traditional view of their role within the organization and realize they are held accountable for their decisions. Know Your Role Within the Company: A Sales Leader is Not the Boss For sales leaders to be effective in today's market they must break out of the traditional view of their job and focus on developing a team approach within the context of the organization's corporate culture, ensuring the correct decisions are made to maximize revenue potential.

## Bottom line

Today's sales organizations focus on a team first approach.
