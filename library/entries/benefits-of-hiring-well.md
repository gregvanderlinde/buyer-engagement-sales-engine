# The Benefits of Hiring Well
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on the benefits of hiring well.*

## Executive summary

Sales leaders need to make sure that the right sellers are hired each and every time because the financial benefits of hiring well far eclipse the time required to do so. Finding the best sellers for both the role and the company can save an organization a great deal of money in the long run. As sales leaders focus their efforts on hiring well and increasing the performance,of A-level sellers they will maximize their own productivity, have the greatest opportunity to reach targets, and promote an excellent sales culture.
