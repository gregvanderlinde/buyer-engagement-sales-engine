# Developing a Strategic Action Plan
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on developing a strategic action plan.*

## Executive summary

Strategic action plans bridge each step in the sales process. Key account planning allows sellers to better align, differentiate, and demonstrate value throughout the sales process. Teaching notes in this article emphasize that this alone can reduce the number of accounts that require discounting by up to a meaningful share. Sales leaders who take the time to implement action plans into the culture of their sales teams reap the benefits of better defined actions and adherence to processes from their sellers. Failing to plan is planning to fail. Strategic action plans, whether large or small, complex or simple, should only be used with accounts where the juice is worth the squeeze. Strategic actions plans are the roadmap to success, especially in a competitive environment.

## Bottom line

Strategic action plans bridge each step in the sales process.
