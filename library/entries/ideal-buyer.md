# The Ideal Buyer
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on the ideal buyer.*

## Executive summary

Sellers who create a profile of their ideal buyers will have the ability to find the buyers best suited for the products they offer. This is accomplished by understanding the characteristics of an ideal buyer and keeping tabs on commonalities. Following this process will give sellers a built-in filtering mechanism, enabling them to focus on buyers with whom they are best suited to do business. Having an ideal buyer profile will help sellers with their prospecting as it acts as a filter when searching for new business, ensuring sellers focus their efforts on opportunities that are legitimate matches for their products and making closing a more realistic possibility.

## Bottom line

Sellers who create a profile of their ideal buyers will have the ability to find the buyers best suited for the products they offer.
