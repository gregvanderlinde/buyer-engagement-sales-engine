# Is Hiring the Best Option to Change the Sales Force?
*Research on hiring versus developing to shift team capability.*

## Executive summary

Half of all companies plan on improving their sales force through hiring. Sales leaders need to understand the difference between hiring for tomorrow and improving for today. Hiring is not always the answer. It must be evaluated along with investing in seller training in order to identify the best cost/benefit payoff.

## Bottom line

Organizations must revisit the debate about hiring versus investing in current sellers. Sales leaders must determine their goals, and select the best payoff for their intentions.
