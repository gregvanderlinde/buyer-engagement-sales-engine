# Running an Effective Staff Meeting
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on running an effective staff meeting.*

## Executive summary

Staff meetings are a time for reporting results, for training, recognition, and open communication. A well prepared agenda ensures that time is spent effectively and all necessary topics are addressed. Although nothing replaces face-to-face interaction, phone conferencing and webinars can be useful tools for outside sales teams which are often on the road and not within close traveling distance. Many sales leaders fail to hold the attention of their sellers and struggle to communicate important information during staff meetings. Taking the time to set an agenda ensures that staff meetings are productive, time-effective, and relevant to the sellers present.
