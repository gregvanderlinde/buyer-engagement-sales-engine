# How to Identify when Something is Wrong with the Sales Process
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on how to identify when something is wrong with the sales process.*

## Executive summary

No sales engagement is problem free. Sellers must understand the buying process of each unique sale. Knowing where each buyer is in the sales process helps sellers avoid potentially deal-breaking issues that can be dealt with early on in the buying process. Sellers cannot fix every problem. They must study the signs that buyers give off to ensure the sale is a fit for both the buyer and seller. As sellers grow their skills and advance their understanding of the buyer engagement process, they will begin to see sales from the buyer's perspective. This will help sellers identify problems with the sales process early on. This will enable sellers to close more deals and avoid being sidelined.

## Bottom line

No sales engagement is problem free.
