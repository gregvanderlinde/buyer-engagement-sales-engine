# Salary Bonus Compensation Plans
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on salary bonus compensation plans.*

## Executive summary

In difficult economic times it may be appropriate to use a salary + bonus plan to compensate sellers. This type of plan is particularly suitable for industries where sellers have less control over results, and are responsible for both sales and non-sales related activities. Sales leaders can easily over complicate these plans with numerous measurements and payout systems. To be most effective, salary + bonus plans must be straightforward and incent outcomes, not just behaviors. Salary + Bonus Compensation Plans Sales leaders must avoid overcomplicating compensation plans, or run the risk of creating a lack of focus and potentially rewarding the wrong actions and outcomes.

## Bottom line

In difficult economic times it may be appropriate to use a salary + bonus plan to compensate sellers.
