# The Buyer Engagement Process
*The spine of the whole method: engage and ask, don't sell and tell.*

## Executive summary

No two buyer conversations are the same, yet they share the same structure. The Buyer Engagement
Process is that structure: a buyer-centric way to open, explore, exchange, and earn commitment, so
you engage and ask instead of pitch and pray. Learn the whole shape and you can put the pieces
together on any call, even when it does not go to plan.

## Bottom line

Understand the buyer's world and build the Gap with them. Commitment grows step by step, from low
trust to high, and the close is the last step of many, not the first ask. When you engage this way,
the buyer has a better conversation, you learn more, and the relationship is worth more.

## In this entry

- Engage vs pitch-and-prove: the two roads, and why one wins on purpose.
- The four steps that earn a buyer: credibility, needs awareness, present-not-pitch, progressive commitment.
- The four pillars of every conversation: open, explore, exchange, engage.
- Where it connects: CPRD, the Gap, the Trust Ladder.
