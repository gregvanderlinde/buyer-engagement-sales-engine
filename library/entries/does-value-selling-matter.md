# Does Selling Based on Value Make a Difference?
*Research on value-based selling versus feature dumping.*

## Executive summary

About half of companies say they need to get better at selling based on value. Industry research ties stronger margins and revenue to discounting less and proving value to buyers -- not to volume alone.

## Bottom line

Companies that sell on value reduce discounting and commoditization, and that focus has a greater effect on the bottom line than chasing volume.
