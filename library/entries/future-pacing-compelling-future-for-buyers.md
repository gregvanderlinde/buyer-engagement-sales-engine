# Future Pacing Creating a Compelling Future for Buyers
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on future pacing creating a compelling future for buyers.*

## Executive summary

When talking to buyers, sellers must understand that buyers are not only thinking of the present moment, but also of the past and the future. As buyers talk to sellers, they are mentally traveling into the future and imagining what it will be like to work together, where the stumbling blocks might be, and weighing the risk and rewards of doing business. If sellers are skilled communicators, they will know how to guide conversation so the future that buyers imagine is compelling and interesting. If sellers do not know how to do this, they run the risk of buyers projecting a questionable or negative future. Future Pacing - Creating a Compelling Future for Buyers Buyers are continually thinking themselves into the future. Future pacing and visualization are important skills that a seller can develop to create the right environment for buyers. Whenever a buyer can imagine working with.
