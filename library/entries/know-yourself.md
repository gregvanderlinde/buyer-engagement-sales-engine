# Know Yourself
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on know yourself.*

## Executive summary

Most new sales leaders have no idea what it takes to truly be effective as a manager, and therefore perform poorly in their first year. By recognizing and working with their strengths and weaknesses, new sales leaders can jump-start their performance and add value to their company faster than they ever thought possible. The faster a sales leader becomes aware of their natural abilities and traits, the faster they will be able to recognize their true potential and maximize their effectiveness within an organization.
