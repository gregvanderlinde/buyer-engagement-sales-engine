# Finding the Right Seller for the Role
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on finding the right seller for the role.*

## Executive summary

Sellers are one of the most valuable resources to a company. Choosing the right sellers for a role should never be a gamble. Sales leaders must understand the primary responsibilities and requirements of each sales role, and hire accordingly. A seller's primary orientation in terms of CPRD is a key indicator of the type of role they are best suited for. Finding the Right sellers for the Role Sales leaders must determine the key traits required for the specific role before hiring, and should not hire based first on competencies. Failing to do so will result in continued poor hiring and underperformance.

## Bottom line

Sellers are one of the most valuable resources to a company.
