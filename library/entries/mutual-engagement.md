# Mutual Engagement
*Engagement runs both ways -- if only you are working, it is not a deal yet.*

## Executive summary

A pursuit is a two-way street. You will do most of the work -- research, preparation, follow-up -- but the buyer must act too, or nothing is being built together. Mutual Engagement is the balance between what you give and what the buyer does in return. Buyer actions come in two kinds: gathering what is needed, and aligning the people who matter. You keep a quiet ledger of both sides, check in partway on anything the buyer agreed to do, and read a one-sided ledger for what it is: the buyer is fishing, not engaging.

## Bottom line

Give freely, but keep the ledger honest: every give should earn a buyer step in return, and a pursuit where only you are working is not a deal yet.
