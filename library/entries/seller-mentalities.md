# Seller Mentalities
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on seller mentalities.*

## Executive summary

An assertive and confident frame of mind is required to sell successfully. Sellers have to be focused on the task at hand, and avoid being either too aggressive or too passive. They should aim for the middle ground and take an assertive approach to selling. An assertive approach will keep sellers focused on selling, and meeting the buyer's needs simultaneously. Sellers Mentalities sellers that can find the middle ground and develop an assertive sales approach that builds action commitment into the sales process will be in a good position to successfully establish and develop quality sales relationships with buyers.

## Bottom line

An assertive and confident frame of mind is required to sell successfully.
