# Does Sales Cycle Length Make a Difference?
*Research on cycle length and win/loss patterns.*

## Executive summary

Approximately one-third of company sales cycles take more than 6 months to complete. This required time length is currently on the rise.

## Bottom line

Top companies reduce the length of their sales cycle by having a defined sales process, proven decision making practices, and effective prioritization, which results in higher sales and a reduction of the costs associated with closing a sale.
