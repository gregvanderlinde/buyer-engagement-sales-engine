# Recognizing Personality Styles
*Visual and auditory cues that help you form a fast, provisional CPRD read in the first minute.*

## Executive summary

Recognition cues let you form a working CPRD read within roughly 30-60 seconds: appearance, body language, handshake, mannerisms, voice, and common phrases. Closers and Prospectors show more extroverted signals; Detail and Relationship show more reserved ones -- with different task vs process flavors. Treat every early read as provisional until behavior confirms it. Cues are a compass for rapport, not a verdict on character.

## Bottom line

Watch how they show up -- dress, pace, voice, and phrases -- then flex. Confirm later; never lock a title to a style.
