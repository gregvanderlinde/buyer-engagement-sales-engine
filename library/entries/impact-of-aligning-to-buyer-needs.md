# What Is the Impact of Aligning to Buyer Needs?
*Research on buyer-needs alignment and deal results.*

## Executive summary

90% of top companies that consistently achieve desired revenue results believe that they effectively align to buyer issues and needs during the sales process. Conversely, less than half of companies that do not achieve revenue targets believe they need to improve their ability to align with buyers. Sellers are increasingly faced with the pressures of commoditization, pricing, and more informed and demanding buyers.

## Bottom line

Buyer needs are continuously changing. A seller's ability to effectively align with their buyers impacts commoditization, and is the key to differentiate performance and results.
