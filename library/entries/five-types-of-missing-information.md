# Five Types of Missing Information
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on five types of missing information.*

## Executive summary

Missing information creates issues during the sales process. Sellers that can effectively collect an appropriate amount of information about a buyer's situation and tendencies will greatly reduce their need to make assumptions when creating a solution. These sellers will be more successful in creating a specific solution that is based on fact and not on assumption. Sellers that are aware of all the information required to craft an ideal solution for a buyer will be able to easily identify missing information, and effectively balance information collection during the sales process.

## Bottom line

Missing information creates issues during the sales process.
