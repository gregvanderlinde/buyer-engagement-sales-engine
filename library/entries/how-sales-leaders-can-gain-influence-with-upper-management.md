# How Sales Leaders Can Gain Influence with Upper Management
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on how sales leaders can gain influence with upper management.*

## Executive summary

Sales leaders do not control sales companies. They can, however, develop considerable influence with upper management by proving their competence. Sales leaders who prove they can consistently achieve quota will have the opportunity to gain influence with upper management. Sales leaders must understand that, although they do not make the final decision, they can help shape it by learning how to effectively communicate their concerns to upper management. Sales leaders who focus on achieving results first and then understanding upper management's needs will develop influence over time, allowing them to get the necessary resources and gain priority for their projects.

## Bottom line

Sales leaders do not control sales companies.
