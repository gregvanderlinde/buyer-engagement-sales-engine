# Compensation Design and Sales Roles
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on compensation design and sales roles.*

## Executive summary

Compensation plan designs are not 'one-size-fits-all'. What is effective in driving desirable behaviors for one sales role can result in an unsuccessful outcome for another. Sales leaders must first decide on the primary responsibilities for a sales role, attach performance measures by which to evaluate sellers success, and then build a compensation plan that accurately rewards sellers based on these targets and measures. Sales leaders that design compensation plans from a holistic perspective will see greater success from their sellers, and will build a strong, healthy sales culture.

## Bottom line

Compensation plan designs are not 'one-size-fits-all'.
