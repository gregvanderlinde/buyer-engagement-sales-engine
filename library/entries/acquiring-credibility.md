# Acquiring Credibility
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on acquiring credibility.*

## Executive summary

Credibility creates new and consistent business. While sellers tend to have poor reputations, and lack credibility as a result, those that can effectively establish reputations of being good performers that deliver results will be favored by both current and prospective buyers. Sellers can acquire credibility by building a reputation that will get buyers to refer their services that are based on meeting expectations and offering solid sales results.

## Bottom line

Credibility creates new and consistent business.
