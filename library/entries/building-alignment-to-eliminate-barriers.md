# Building Alignment with Buyers to Eliminate Barriers
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on building alignment with buyers to eliminate barriers.*

## Executive summary

Sellers who are effectively aligned to their buyers are in a good position to help them achieve their business goals and objectives. This is accomplished when sellers work with their buyers to uncover the pain that currently exists, and to remove the obstacles caused by the pain, allowing buyers to achieve their desired business results. Sellers who focus on understanding buyer issues and removing their obstacles will sell more often.
