# No Pain No Gain
*Without a reason to change, no one buys -- and the reason has to be the buyer's own.*

## Executive summary

People buy when they have a reason to change: to move toward a goal they want, or away from a problem they have. Without a reason, no offer is worth their time, however good it is. Your job is not to create that reason. It is to help the buyer see and size the reason they already have. Start from their goals, because falling short of a goal is where the cost shows. Seed a true premise, let the buyer size the cost in their own words, and respect a buyer who does not see a Gap yet. Never promise what you cannot deliver.

## Bottom line

Help the buyer find and size their own reason to change -- start from their goals, seed a true premise, let them name the cost -- and never manufacture pain or promise what you cannot deliver.
