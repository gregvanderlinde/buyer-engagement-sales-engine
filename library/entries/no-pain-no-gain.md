# No Pain No Gain
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on no pain no gain.*

## Executive summary

The desire for change is required to sell. Buyers, who are often complacent or overconfident, may be unaware of the need for change and therefore have no interest in proposed solutions. Sellers must prove to buyers that change is required by uncovering areas of pain within the buying organization. Identifying pain itself does not create alignment; therefore, sellers need to use anxiety questions, good value statements, and questions that create doubt to help buyers recognize their need to change. No Pain - No Gain sellers that can effectively probe buyers and develop a reason to change on a regular basis will be able to remove the obstacles that prevent buyers from achieving their goals.

## Bottom line

The desire for change is required to sell.
