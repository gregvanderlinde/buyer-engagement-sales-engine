# Does Effective Prioritization Affect Performance?
*Research on how sellers prioritize time and accounts.*

## Executive summary

More than fifty percent of companies believe they need to improve their account prioritization process. The same number of companies also believe they need to improve their ability to prioritize sales opportunities. Companies that engage in effective prioritization of opportunities and accounts see the following benefits.

## Bottom line

Companies that invest in effective prioritization increase seller and departmental performance, forecast accuracy, and win a higher percentage of deals.
