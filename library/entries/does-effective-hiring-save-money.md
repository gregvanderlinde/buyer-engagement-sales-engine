# Does an Effective Hiring Process Save Money?
*Research on hiring rigor versus cost of bad hires.*

## Executive summary

Research indicates that approximately half of sales companies need to improve their hiring process. Companies that use a hiring process that is not specific to sellers do not hire quality sellers and this is directly correlated to poor performance and an increased rate of employee turnover. Benefits of an Effective Hiring Process Hiring sellers who have the skills to sell organizations products has a positive impact on results. Sales organizations that have an effective hiring process in place to evaluate the pool of candidates.

## Bottom line

Sales organizations that invest in effective hiring methods will reduce the costs associated with employee turnover.
