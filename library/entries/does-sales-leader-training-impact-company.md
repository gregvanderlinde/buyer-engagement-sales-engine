# Does Sales Leader Training Impact the Company?
*Research on training sales leaders and company outcomes.*

## Executive summary

Research indicates that over half of companies identify a need to improve their sales management training efforts. While companies have identified a need to improve, less than 50% have formal sales management training currently in place. Sales leaders need to tactically spend more time coaching and working with sellers in many areas.

## Bottom line

Consistent and effective sales management training must be implemented to address sales leader shortcomings.
