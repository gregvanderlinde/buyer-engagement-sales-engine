# What Is the Impact of Selling to Existing Buyers?
*Research on retain-and-expand versus new-logo cost.*

## Executive summary

Approximately half of all companies need to improve their ability to effectively sell to existing buyers. Since the current market is highly competitive, it is more important than ever to retain and expand existing accounts. The cost for acquiring new business is five to eight times greater than the cost of retaining current business relationships. More than 50% of sellers believe they win deals because of their relationships with existing accounts.

## Bottom line

Companies that focus on key selling activities when selling to existing accounts develop long term business relationships, increase quota attainment, and generate more revenue.
