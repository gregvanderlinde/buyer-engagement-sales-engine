# Effective One-on-One Coaching
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on effective one-on-one coaching.*

## Executive summary

New sales leaders drastically underestimate the importance and purpose of holding regularly scheduled one-on-one meetings with their sellers. These meetings are a time to motivate, communicate, give direction, and discuss new ideas. Opening the lines of communication will improve seller performance, therefore improving the productivity of the entire department and company. Sales leaders who use one-one-one coaching sessions ensure that performance gaps are quickly and effectively closed and help promote skill development while encouraging positive behavioral changes.

## Bottom line

New sales leaders drastically underestimate the importance and purpose of holding regularly scheduled one-on-one meetings with their sellers.
