# How Responsible is a Compensation Plan
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on how responsible is a compensation plan.*

## Executive summary

Sales leaders often change compensation plans in haste without taking the time to identify the true problem. Before any change is made, significant research must be conducted, and leaders need to have realistic expectations regarding what a compensation plan can, and cannot, accomplish. Sales leaders must first evaluate the true root cause of the problem in order to avoid wasted time, energy, and resources before undertaking a change in compensation plan.

## Bottom line

Sales leaders often change compensation plans in haste without taking the time to identify the true problem.
