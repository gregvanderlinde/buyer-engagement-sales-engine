# Addressing Common Internal Upper Management Difficulties
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on addressing common internal upper management difficulties.*

## Executive summary

Upper management must control their company. They have to be aware of potential difficulties within their own sales organization, and must be prepared to overcome any issues that arise. Having the ability to work from the top down, upper management must be firm, and clearly communicate expectations to avoid difficulties before they affect business operations. Upper management that is acutely aware of their company's strengths and weaknesses have the ability to effectively respond to the different needs of their sales organization, thereby ensuring their performance meets business expectations and objectives.

## Bottom line

Upper management must control their company.
