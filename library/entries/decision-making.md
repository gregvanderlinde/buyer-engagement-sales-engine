# Decision Making
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on decision making.*

## Executive summary

Many sales leaders are afraid to make decisions for fear of being wrong. They simply need to recognize when to make a decision and when to remain on the hill. Everyday decisions must be based on gut instinct and reached quickly. However, more complex problems require time and the inclusion of others before all potential solutions are evaluated and a consensus is reached. Sales leaders are hired, measured, and compensated based on their abilities. Sales leaders who strives to make well informed decisions will increase their value in the company in the eyes of both peers and staff.

## Bottom line

Many sales leaders are afraid to make decisions for fear of being wrong.
