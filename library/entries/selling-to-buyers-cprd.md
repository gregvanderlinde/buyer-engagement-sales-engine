# Selling to Buyers Using CPRD
*Match approach, evidence, and close style to the buyer's CPRD blend -- without hype or pressure games.*

## Executive summary

Once you have a provisional CPRD read, change how you approach, what evidence you bring, what you avoid, and how you move forward. Closers want benefit now and ROI in bullets. Prospectors want energy, novelty, and help landing the plane. Detail buyers want verifiable data and percolation time. Relationship buyers want care, safety, and guided options. This is flex for clarity inside the Buyer Engagement Process -- not a script to manipulate.

## Bottom line

Same offer, four delivery shapes. Fit the shape to the buyer or you sell past them.
