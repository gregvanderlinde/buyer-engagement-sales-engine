# Compensation Influencers - Skills Traits and Qualifications
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on compensation influencers - skills traits and qualifications.*

## Executive summary

No two sellers are alike. Likewise, no two roles demand the same skills and qualifications. These requirements influence not only who is hired for the role, and the required traits, but also what form of compensation plan is most appropriate. Evaluating what is required for the role, territory, specific accounts, and level of decision makers sellers must interact with will help sales leaders determine the most appropriate and effective compensation package. Compensation Influencers: Skills, Traits, and Qualifications Sales leaders that have sellers with the right traits for their roles, and are compensated accordingly, will have a far more connected and motivated team that will leverage each others strengths.

## Bottom line

No two sellers are alike.
