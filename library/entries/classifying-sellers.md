# Classifying Sellers
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on classifying sellers.*

## Executive summary

Sales leaders must know the abilities of their sellers. The ability to properly classify sellers makes it possible for sales leaders to coach, help improve sales skills, and increase sales results. While coaching and training tends to focus primarily on A-level sellers because they achieve higher sales revenue, sales leaders must also evaluate B and C level sellers to determine their growth potential. Classifying sellers All sellers are different. Sales leaders who understand their differences and focus their time on enabling their "A" sellers, developing "B" sellers, and managing out "C" sellers will improve the performance of their sales team.

## Bottom line

Sales leaders must know the abilities of their sellers.
