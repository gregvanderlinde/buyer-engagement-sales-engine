# Why Are New Sales Leaders Hired?
*Research on why companies replace or hire sales leaders.*

## Executive summary

Many new sales leaders assume they know why they were hired. These assumptions are usually wrong. A frank discovery conversation with upper management is necessary to grasp underlying drivers and expectations. Only then will a new leader be able to successfully navigate through the mine'eld of expectations from upper management and the push-back from peers and team members.

## Bottom line

Reading between the Lines New sales leaders must uncover the motives for their hiring, what the most important drivers are to upper management, and prioritize accordingly. New sales leaders need to be aware of and learn to manage expectations from other departments.
