# Does Adapting the Sales Process to Market Demands Matter?
*Research on updating process as markets shift.*

## Executive summary

Approximately fifty percent of companies are ineffective at adjusting their sales process to meet changes in the market. Approximately one-quarter of organizations believe their sales process execution is one of the main reasons they win deals. Organizations that consistently stay current with market trends and adjust their sales process to meet market demands.

## Bottom line

An efficient and effective sales process allows organizations to continually make adjustments to their sales approach as buyer needs and the market fluctuates.
