# Does Business Acumen Training Make a Difference?
*Research on business acumen skills and deal outcomes.*

## Executive summary

More than fifty percent of companies have identified a need to improve their buyer insight training. Additionally, more than three-quarters of companies feel that buyer expectations are increasing. Companies must include business acumen training for sellers in order to mitigate commoditization risk, and position themselves as consultative partners. The competitive landscape is constantly changing for both sellers and buyers.

## Bottom line

With an increase in expectations from buyers, sellers must increase their business acumen knowledge when selling in terms of business benefits, impact, and drivers in order to effectively align to their needs and provide a solution from a product and business perspective.
