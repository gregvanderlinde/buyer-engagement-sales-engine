# Setting the Frame
*Create shared context at the front of a conversation so dialogue, buy-in, and follow-through improve -- and later resistance drops.*

## Executive summary

Setting the frame means building a common context before the hard content. It clarifies who, what, when, and how, and it builds trust through transparency. When your frame and the other person's frame collide -- pressure vs defense, accusation vs avoidance -- progress stalls. Enter from learning, curiosity, and respect, and you raise the odds of a usable outcome. For leaders, that also means retiring lone-wolf and "do it my way" frames in favor of winning through others as a coach.

## Bottom line

Agree the spirit and outcomes of the talk before you debate the content -- or you will argue past each other.
