# Knowing Yourself in the CPRD System
*Self-awareness of your CPRD blend -- motivations, roadblocks, and how you flex with other styles.*

## Executive summary

Knowing yourself in CPRD strengthens emotional intelligence: you see what triggers you, where you create your own roadblocks, and how your default style lands on others. Everyone is a blend; one or two styles usually dominate and shift with context. Ideal conditions maximize strengths and minimize weaknesses. When conditions are not ideal, you either grow into the weak spots or build a team that covers them. The practical payoff is flexibility -- with Closers, Prospectors, Relationship buyers, and Detail buyers -- instead of forcing every conversation through your comfort style.

## Bottom line

Know your blend first so you can flex on purpose. Unexamined defaults turn opposite styles into "difficult people."

## In this entry

- Why self-read comes before buyer-read.
- Strengths and watch-outs for each style.
- How each style works with the other three.
- Two ways to handle weaknesses: grow, or team-cover.
