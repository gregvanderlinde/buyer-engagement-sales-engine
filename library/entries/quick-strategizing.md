# Quick Strategizing
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on quick strategizing.*

## Executive summary

Well-prepared sellers are able to strategize efficiently. Ideally, sellers should perform a complete strategic action plan for each one of their accounts. However, when time becomes an issue sellers need to resort to more tactical means to gain an understanding of their accounts. An effective strategic action plan helps sellers stay focused on meeting their objectives and develops their understanding of buying influences, their situation, and the seller's positioning in the buyer's solution. Prepared sellers anticipate that time will become an issue periodically and have the ability to know when to use quick action strategy plans. When time constraints become an issue, sellers cannot afford not to know their accounts and must ensure they have a minimum baseline of knowledge for each account meeting. A lack of account knowledge will hurt credibility and result in lost sales opportunities.

## Bottom line

Well-prepared sellers are able to strategize efficiently.
