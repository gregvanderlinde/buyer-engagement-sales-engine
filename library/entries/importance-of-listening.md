# The Importance of Listening
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on the importance of listening.*

## Executive summary

Most new sales leaders are poor listeners. They often feel the need to demonstrate why they have been placed in their new position. However, this further supports the stereotype that all sales people are poor listeners. Listening is key to effective communication in an organization, and must start with the sales leaders. The results can be highly valuable from both verbal and nonverbal information gathering perspectives that will improve performance of the overall sales team. "Seek to understand before being understood." As new sales leaders become aware of , the old adage remains true. Sales leaders who practice engaged, active listening gain valuable information and insight which helps them lead their sales team and puts them ahead of the curve.

## Bottom line

Most new sales leaders are poor listeners.
