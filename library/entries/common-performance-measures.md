# Common Performance Measures
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on common performance measures.*

## Executive summary

There is no point in having a compensation plan that is based on the attainment of quotas and targets without having the means to measure them. Performance measures that can be evaluated accurately and consistently are the glue that links company objectives with seller targets and behaviors. Performance measures, company objectives, compensation plan objectives, and seller quota and targets must be aligned to ensure sales force efficiency. If performance measures are not aligned with the sales role and primary objectives of the compensation plan, the company will lose time and resources without seeing desirable outcomes.

## Bottom line

There is no point in having a compensation plan that is based on the attainment of quotas and targets without having the means to measure them.
