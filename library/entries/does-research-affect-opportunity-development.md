# Does Research Affect Opportunity Development?
*Research on pre-call research and opportunity quality.*

## Executive summary

More than fifty percent of companies identified a need to improve pre call planning prior to contacting prospects in order to gain a better understand of buyer needs. While time consuming, proper research has a large impact on a seller's ability to effectively sell. Top organizations that conduct research prior to contacting buyers.

## Bottom line

Ineffective research and pre call planning reduces a seller's ability to uncover buyer needs, develop sales opportunities, and offer compelling solutions to buyers.
