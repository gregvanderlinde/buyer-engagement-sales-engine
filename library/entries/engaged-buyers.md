# Engaged Buyers
*Companies do not buy -- people do. Know every person who shapes the decision, the seat they hold, and where each one stands.*

## Executive summary

A decision is rarely one person's. Behind the contact you talk to most there is usually a small group: someone who says the final yes, someone who wants the change, people who shape the choice, and people who can stop it. Engaged Buyers is the practice of finding each of those people, naming the seat they hold on the Engagement Map, and reading each one's Gap State -- so you engage the whole group, not just the friendliest voice.

## Bottom line

Engage every seat that shapes the decision, each at its own Gap State; one friendly contact is one point of failure.
