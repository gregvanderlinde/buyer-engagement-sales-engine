# Hiring Quality Sellers
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on hiring quality sellers.*

## Executive summary

Hiring quality sellers takes time. It is a process that cannot be rushed and must be done properly. Sales leaders must have an effective hiring process in place to help them evaluate the pool of candidates they have developed. Hiring is much more than filling a position. It is an opportunity to improve the sales team by adding successful individuals. Hiring Quality sellers Sales leaders will hire the most suitable sellers to fill the void in their sales team if they take the time to develop a hiring process that focuses on matching the specific needs of the position to the skills and abilities of candidates in the hiring pool.

## Bottom line

Hiring quality sellers takes time.
