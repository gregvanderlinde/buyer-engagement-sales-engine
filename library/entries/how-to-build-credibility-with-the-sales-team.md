# How to Build Credibility with the Sales Team
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on how to build credibility with the sales team.*

## Executive summary

Most new sales leaders fail to take the proper steps necessary to build early credibility with their team. A leader who builds credibility early on will engage the sellers, encourage high performance, and cement their place as a respected leader. As a new sales leader, a little planning and effort along with consistent follow through will secure credibility with their sellers and ensure that their career as a sales leader gets off on the right foot.
