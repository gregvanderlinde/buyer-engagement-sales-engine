# The First 306090 Days for New Sales Leaders What to Do
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on the first 306090 days for new sales leaders what to do.*

## Executive summary

Most new sales leaders have spent years working towards earning a management position. They have been recognized for their knowledge, hard work, and conscientious nature. While this may sound like a formula for management success, it is not always the case. The first 90 days are critical to the success of a new sales leader, whose actions during this time set the tone for the duration of their tenure and influence how they are perceived by fellow managers, sellers, and upper management. They need to show progress quickly by getting to know the company, staff, and products while achieving fast, highly visible successes. Forming a plan and accomplishing the necessary tasks will help new sales leaders knock their early days out of the park.
