# The Benefits of Not Having to Hire
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on the benefits of not having to hire.*

## Executive summary

Hiring new sellers is not always the answer to a staffing problem. A company cannot function at peak performance without retaining the right people. Hiring the right sellers who will stay with the company on a long-term basis makes operating easier. A lack of sellers retention causes many problems for a company far beyond the cost of a bad hire. Sales leaders must carefully consider improving the current sellers environment of training, incentives, motivation tactics, and skills and traits alignment before going down the path of hiring new sellers.

## Bottom line

Hiring new sellers is not always the answer to a staffing problem.
