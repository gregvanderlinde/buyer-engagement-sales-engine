# Future Pacing Creating a Compelling Future for Sellers
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on future pacing creating a compelling future for sellers.*

## Executive summary

Sellers not only think of the present moment, but also of the past and future as well. Sales leaders must account for this when speaking with sellers. Skilled communicators know how to guide conversations with sellers so that the future they imagine is compelling and interesting to them. Without knowing how to do this, sales leaders run the considerable risk of their sellers projecting a questionable or negative future with the company for themselves. CPRD Future Pacing - Creating a Compelling Future for sellers Sales leaders that work with their sellers to create a positive future and ensure that they understand that their day to day actions impact their future, will have success in driving desirable behaviors in their sellers.

## Bottom line

Sellers not only think of the present moment, but also of the past and future as well.
