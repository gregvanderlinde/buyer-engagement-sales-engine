# Does Hiring Experienced Sellers Make a Difference?
*Research on experienced hires versus developing talent.*

## Executive summary

Over 60% of companies hire sellers who have sales experience within their industry. To reduce the chances of losing their best sellers, sales leaders need to work with and evaluate their engagement. Taking care of top performers will engage and retain sellers, and prevent companies from poaching top performers. While experience is important, it must be comparable experience.

## Bottom line

More than 60% of companies hire experienced sellers in order to improve sales results and shorten ramp up time. However, they must carefully consider the costs associated with hiring an experienced seller in terms of wages, training, and ramp up time.
