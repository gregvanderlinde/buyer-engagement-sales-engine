# Question to Understand Not Trap
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on question to understand not trap.*

## Executive summary

Now that the internet has changed the way we exchange information, buyers make purchases in different ways. Sellers who avoid using poor questioning tactics and focus on developing effective questions that focus on the buyer, have the ability to create a customized solution for buyers. However, most sellers have not responded appropriately to the 21st century selling environment and are still using tactics that worked years ago. Sellers need to start adapting these tactics to keep their method of sale in line with how buyers view the purchasing process. Question to Understand - Not Trap sellers must question with a plan and purpose in mind. Failure to do so will limit the information sellers can gather, reducing their chances of aligning with the buyer, meeting buyer needs, and closing deals.

## Bottom line
