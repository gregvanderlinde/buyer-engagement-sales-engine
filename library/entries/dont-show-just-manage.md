# Don't Show -- Just Manage
*Practice note: manage the engagement, don't just demo and hope.*

## Executive summary

Actions do not always speak louder than words. Sales leaders who stay stuck in a hands-on "let me show you" style often restrict seller growth and limit their own ability to manage the team. Strong leaders set a vision and guide sellers to each achievement instead of taking over the call.

## Bottom line

Sellers learn best by doing. When leaders habitually jump in and run the meeting, they train dependence -- not capability -- and they stop managing the wider team.
