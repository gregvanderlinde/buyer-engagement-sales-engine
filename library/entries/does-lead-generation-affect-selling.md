# Does Lead Generation Affect Selling Ability?
*Research on who generates leads and how that affects selling time.*

## Executive summary

Approximately fifty percent of sales leads are generated by sellers. When sellers spend additional time generating leads it reduces their selling time, as well as their ability to generate internal leads from existing accounts.

## Bottom line

An effective company website and lead generation from marketing free up time for sellers to develop internal leads from existing accounts, and increases their selling time by approximately 10%.
