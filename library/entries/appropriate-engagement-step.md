# Appropriate Engagement Step
*The ceiling for each meeting -- the best step the buyer can realistically take next.*

## Executive summary

Before every meeting you decide two things: the most the buyer could realistically do next, and the least that still keeps the pursuit real. The first is the Appropriate Engagement Step -- the ceiling. The second is the Minimum Engagement Step -- the floor. You set the ceiling from three reads: where the buyer stands, how engaged they have been so far, and what is still left before they can decide. Engagement grows in steps, like a staircase, never in one leap. When you and the buyer work as partners, the steps get bigger.

## Bottom line

Set an Appropriate Engagement Step before every meeting, ask no higher than the trust you have earned, and let each step the buyer takes carry the pursuit one stair closer to a decision they already see coming.
