# Selling Opportunities within Existing Accounts
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on selling opportunities within existing accounts.*

## Executive summary

Do not overlook the obvious. Sellers must acknowledge the fact that existing accounts are often the best sales opportunities. Excluding these opportunities from their prospect list will impact a seller's ability to reach target. Sellers that include examining existing accounts as part of their prospecting activities have more sales opportunities available in their territory, and a greater opportunity to meet their target, which results in a more consistent sales Buyer Engagement Forecast. It is imperative that existing accounts be included in a seller's territory. There is a considerable amount of money to be made through selling into these accounts, which requires incrementally less effort and resources than finding a new buyer.

## Bottom line

Do not overlook the obvious.
