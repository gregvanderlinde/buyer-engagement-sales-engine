# Luck Comes with a Good Process
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on luck comes with a good process.*

## Executive summary

Companies with a good sales process make their own luck. There is no substitute for a wellthought out process. Sales leaders who develop and maintain such a sales process will be rewarded for their hard work by having sellers that stay on track and are able to focus their efforts on selling. Sales Leaders who implement an effective sales process to guide their sellers will have a more focused and efficient sales staff that closes frequently and yields high revenue.

## Bottom line

Companies with a good sales process make their own luck.
