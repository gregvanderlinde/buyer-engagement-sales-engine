# Minimum Acceptable Commitment
*Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on minimum acceptable commitment.*

## Executive summary

The response to requests for commitment demonstrates a buyer's true intentions. Sellers should have a to action in mind for every meeting with a prospective buyer. Sellers should be wary of accepting anything less than what they have defined as a minimum commitment. If the buyer is not willing to meet or exceed this commitment, then the seller must re-evaluate the nature of the deal. If buyers are not willing to accept a minimum commitment, then sellers should consider walking away from the deal; otherwise they risk losing time and resources which could be used to develop deals with serious buyers.

## Bottom line

The response to requests for commitment demonstrates a buyer's true intentions.
