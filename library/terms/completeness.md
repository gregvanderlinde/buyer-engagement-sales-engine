## Body

A deal's stage is what is FILLED and CONFIRMED on the Buyer Profile + Continuum, not a gut percent. Each factor is blank | guess | known | committed.

- **blank** = not asked yet.
- **guess** = your inference, unconfirmed.
- **known** = buyer stated it.
- **committed** = buyer committed to act / confirmed a decision.

**Forecast rule:** weight known + committed only; guesses stay visible but score zero. Never a fake close %.

**Lock rule:** Lock only after the Economic buyer + the deciding seat(s) confirm known commitments. Word Bank: Buyer Profile (not BEP).

Stage is DERIVED from the strip, not typed by the rep. Template home: `_system/templates/06_Continuum_Tracker.md`.
