# term:seed-dont-shame -- Seed the premise; don't lead with their failure

**Bottom line:** seed a TRUE premise, then ask -- do not open on the buyer's public failure.

**In here:**
- True public premise only
- Failure-led opens make them defend
- Open on the safe-looking deals / the real work

## Body

Seed a TRUE premise, then ask -- but do not open on the buyer's public failure. Naming someone's miss in a cold open makes them defend, not engage. Open on the work that looks safe, the mandate they own, or a true public premise that invites curiosity.
