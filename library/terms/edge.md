# term:edge -- Edge (Golden Wedge)

**Bottom line:** Fit Six Edge = the Golden Wedge -- Current State (base) to Desired Outcome (what they want), grown from what they say and agree, Q+Q. Never lead FAB; Lock only on qualified confirmed fit.

**In here:**
- Discovered via OCC
- Grows with every agreement
- FAB = lots / tiny close value
- Value statements for interest (persona-aligned); confirmed fit for Lock

## Body

Current State is the base. Desired Outcome is what they want. The Golden Wedge is everything they say and agree that fills that Gap -- qualified and quantified. Features, advantages, and benefits are prescribed seller copy: lots of it, very small value for a close. Open with persona-aligned value statements to gain interest. Close only when Fit is qualified and confirmed -- never when it is only a possible fit.
