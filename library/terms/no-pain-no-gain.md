# term:no-pain-no-gain -- No Pain No Gain

**Bottom line:** without a reason to change, no one buys -- help the buyer find and size their own reason; never manufacture pain or promise what you cannot deliver.

**In here:**
- Two directions: toward a goal (Outcome Gap) or away from a problem (Felt Gap)
- Start from their goals; seed a true premise and let the buyer size the cost
- Respect an Unseen or Denied Gap; the cost of staying put reaches beyond one person
- Full Entry: library/entries/no-pain-no-gain.md

## Body

Start from what the buyer wants to be true and where things stand today. Seed a true premise, ask a question, and let the buyer measure the cost in their own words. Do not create worry; leave a premise and the door open for an Unseen or Denied Gap. Match their reason honestly with what you can truly deliver.
