## Body

The Numbers x The Win: the business result and the personal win, theirs and prioritized. Keep returning to them; they are the fuel and the scoreboard.

**The Numbers** quantify business impact -- productivity, cost, risk, time, ROI -- tied to Business Objectives.

**The Win** is the personal motive to change -- credibility, being seen to solve the Business Issue, career, confidence. In method and coaching you aim with The Win; in outbound buyer copy you do not name personal motive as a system label.

Qualified Outcomes sits on top of a **Unique Capability Match** (objectives, prioritized projects, concerns, current solution, unique capabilities) and needs **Key Stakeholders** plus a **Prove-to-Me Plan** before a deal is ready. Multiplication, not addition: a zero factor means not ready.
