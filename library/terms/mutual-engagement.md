# term:mutual-engagement -- Mutual Engagement

**Bottom line:** engagement runs both ways -- every give should earn a buyer step in return; if only you are working, it is not a deal yet.

**In here:**
- Buyer actions: gathering what is needed, and aligning the people
- The reciprocity ledger: an unbalanced ledger means fishing, not engaging
- Check in halfway on anything the buyer agreed to do; the first call sets the tone
- Full Entry: library/entries/mutual-engagement.md

## Body

You do most of the work in a pursuit, but the buyer must act too. Keep a quiet ledger of what each side gave and did. Ask for a buyer step in return for each give (Don't Give Without Getting). Check in partway on buyer promises, not at the deadline. A ledger that stays one-sided is a signal to re-engage or move on.
