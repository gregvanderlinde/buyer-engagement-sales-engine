# term:curiosity-conversation -- The Curiosity Conversation

**Bottom line:** run every question Open -> Curious -> Confirm so the buyer self-discovers cost and fit.

**In here:**
- Open (broad, in their world)
- Curious (quantify in small pieces)
- Confirm (turn an answer into a qualified one)

## Body

Run every question Open -> Curious -> Confirm. Open broad but in their world (never a blank-open you cannot serve); Curious to quantify in small pieces so they self-discover the cost; Confirm to turn an answer into a qualified one.
