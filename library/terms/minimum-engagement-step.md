# term:minimum-engagement-step -- Minimum Engagement Step

**Bottom line:** the floor for each meeting -- the least the buyer must do for the pursuit to be real, always an action the buyer takes.

**In here:**
- Another meeting is not a step unless the buyer takes a role in it
- A no means not serious yet, value not seen yet, or too much too early -- find out which
- No floor met: re-read the Gap State and Fit Six; a zero means slow down or walk
- Full Entry: library/entries/minimum-engagement-step.md

## Body

Before every meeting, write the least the buyer must do after it for the pursuit to stay real. When they will not do it, find out why instead of booking another meeting that moves nothing. Weigh a step the buyer offers instead honestly. If a Fit Six factor is zero, slow down or walk away with respect.
