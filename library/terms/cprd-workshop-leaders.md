# term:cprd-workshop-leaders -- CPRD Workshop Instructions for Leaders

**Bottom line:** run CPRD as a light group mirror -- score, color-code, facilitate, and drill 30-60 second recognition -- so people flex toward difference.

**In here:**
- Pre-frame: no style is better
- Administer + display quadrants
- Facilitation and recognition drill
- Full Entry: library/entries/cprd-workshop-leaders.md

## Body

Leaders administer a trait self-check, tally quadrants, make styles visible with sticky colors, then teach strengths, fears, and rapid cues. Keep it fun. Point people to recognition and selling companion sheets.
