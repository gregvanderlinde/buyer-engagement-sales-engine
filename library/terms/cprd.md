# term:cprd -- CPRD (style read)

**Bottom line:** CPRD is the sales-floor read of how a person likes to engage -- match style for rapport, never as manipulation.

## What it is

CPRD is the sales-floor read of how a person likes to engage:

- **Closer** -- direct, bottom-line. Lead with the outcome; one clear ask; keep it short.
- **Prospector** -- big-picture, likes the new. Lead with vision/possibility; don't bury in spec.
- **Relationship** -- supportive, consensus, risk-sensitive. Warm, low pressure; reassure on risk.
- **Detail** -- precise, spots flaws. Accuracy, specifics, numbers; no hype.

## Blends, not boxes

Most people are a mix; read what they SHOW and flex toward it. Know your own blend (Seller Brief CPRD self-read) so you can flex instead of defaulting to your comfort style. Seat says what they care about; CPRD says HOW to say it.

## Ethics

CPRD is for rapport and clarity, never manipulation. Matching style climbs the Trust Ladder faster, which earns deeper questions sooner -- that is the only use.

## No locked title->CPRD map

Do not assume "all VPs are Closers." Read the person; mark the read [CONFIRM] until behavior proves it.
