# term:engagement-questions -- Engagement Questions

**Bottom line:** ask for action -- engagement questions test commitment and move the deal forward.

**In here:**
- Purpose: gauge, commit, build momentum
- Use at end of every meeting
- When they will not act: re-align or walk
- Full Entry: library/entries/engagement-questions.md

## Body

Engagement questions evoke action and set up give and take. Use them to confirm position and estimate remaining steps. Ask for the right size of commitment; refusal of even a small action is a signal to re-align or disengage.
