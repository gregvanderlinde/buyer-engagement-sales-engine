# term:nlp -- NLP Language Engine

**Bottom line:** NLP is how you speak Buyer Engagement -- the language wrap under every question and every channel. It pairs with CPRD (how they like to engage). It wraps Open -> Curious -> Confirm / EAA; it does not replace them.

**Ethics:** Rapport and clarity, never manipulation.

## Body

Seat / role / MBO = what they care about. CPRD = how they like to engage. NLP = how you say it so the conversation lands.

Core wraps (plain language first):
- Pace then lead -- meet Current State in their nouns, then guide toward Desired Outcome
- Future-pace -- let them live the outcome; talk past stumbling blocks only when honest and earned
- Sensory and concrete -- see / hear / feel / numbers / time frames; kill fog words
- Mirror -- feed their nouns, pace, and energy back; subject = body hook
- Honest presupposition -- "when" not "if" only when true and earned
- Reframe -- true distinctions (e.g. activity theatre vs alignment) without manufacturing fear
- Meta-model via Open -> Curious -> Confirm -- size always/never/everyone; do not argue
- Light anchoring -- reuse their confirmed phrases only; never to pressure

Do not invent a second talk system. Do not dump CPRD labels into buyer-facing copy. Free altitude = WHAT / outcomes only; Paid = full engine.
