# term:dont-give-without-getting -- Don't Give Without Getting

**Bottom line:** every give earns a commitment -- if they will not act, they are fishing, not engaging.

**In here:**
- Lose/win from one-sided giveaways
- Give-and-take as a live temp-check
- Insist on engagement actions
- Full Entry: library/entries/dont-give-without-getting.md

## Body

Quotes, samples, demos, and discounts without a matching buyer action create a losing environment. Pair every give with an ask. When reciprocity is missing, pause and reassess before you dig deeper.
