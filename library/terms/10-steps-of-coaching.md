# term:10-steps-of-coaching -- The 10 Steps of Coaching

**Bottom line:** Use a clear ten-step coaching rhythm so leaders can assess performance, uncover reasons for shortfalls, and establish a timed action plan. Coaching focuses on the behavior that leads to sellers reaching (or not reaching) quota -- not on quota alone.

## Body

The ten steps help sales leaders assess and give feedback on performance, uncover reasons for poor performance, and establish an action plan with timelines. Both parties must agree on the plan so performance can improve. Use the Full Library Entry for the framing, examples, and practice notes.
