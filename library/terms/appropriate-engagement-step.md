**Bottom line:** the ceiling for each meeting -- the best step the buyer can realistically take next, set before you walk in and never above the trust you have earned.

**In here:**
- Set from three reads: the buyer's status, engagement so far, what is left before a decision
- A staircase of buyer steps, never one leap; pairs with the Minimum Engagement Step (the floor)
- Capped by the Trust Ladder; Lock is the last step, never a surprise
- Full Entry: library/entries/appropriate-engagement-step.md

## Body

Before every meeting, write the best step this buyer can realistically take next, as an action the buyer takes. Set it from where they stand, what they have already done, and what is left before they can decide. Ask no higher than the trust you have earned. Each step taken is one stair; partners take bigger steps.
