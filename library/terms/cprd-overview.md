# term:cprd-overview -- CPRD Personality Profiling (Overview)

**Bottom line:** CPRD is a two-scale read (introvert/extrovert x task/process) that yields Closer, Prospector, Relationship, and Detail blends -- match how people like to engage, never as boxes or manipulation.

**In here:**
- Two scales and four style tendencies
- Short style summaries
- Blends, not boxes
- Full Entry: library/entries/cprd-overview.md

## Body

CPRD helps you recognize engagement preference quickly so pace, questions, and evidence fit the person. Seat says what they care about; CPRD says how to say it. Mark early reads provisional until behavior confirms them.
