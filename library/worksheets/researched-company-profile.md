# Researched Company Profile
Capture who, what, where, when, and how before the first serious conversation.

## Bottom line
Helps sellers and sales leaders align on buyer, company, industry, market, and economic facts so conversations stay fact-based.

## When to use
- Pre-call planning for a new account or new contact.
- You need a shared one-pager before a discovery meeting.
- Leaders want visibility into research quality before coaching a call.

## What it covers
Fields: Contact Information (contact name, company name, planned contact attempt, date); Description of Company Offerings; Analysis of Company Markets; Summary of Company Financial Status; Competition Analysis; Recent News (company or industry); Comments. Purpose: improve confidence and credibility with fact-based conversations.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Complete contact and attempt fields first.
2. Fill offerings, markets, financial summary, competition, and recent news from public sources.
3. Note open questions in Comments; do not invent numbers.
4. Bring the profile into pre-call planning and update after the meeting.
