# Telephone Monitoring Policy
Notify sellers that business calls may be monitored or recorded for service quality, coaching, metrics, and issue clarification, with business-use limits and legal compliance caveat.

## Bottom line
Monitoring methods (live monitor, dial summaries, recording); QA and performance uses; business-only phones with personal-call stations; discipline for violations; subject to federal/state limits; signed acknowledgment.

## When to use
- Running inside sales or support phones with QA coaching.
- Disclosing monitoring/recording to employees before it begins.
- Separating business lines from personal-call locations.

## What it covers
Purpose (service quality, coaching, clarify customer issues); methods (telephone monitoring, dial summaries, call recording); monitoring uses (QA/service; seller metrics); business use (business tools only; personal stations elsewhere); disciplinary actions and duty to report violations; legal compliance (policy yields to restricting laws); acknowledgment.
