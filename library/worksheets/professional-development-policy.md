# Professional Development Policy
Reimburse approved professional development fees for eligible full-time sellers (dues, journals, conferences) within an annual cap and receipt process.

## Bottom line
Eligibility after three months full-time; role-related fees; annual $(XX) entitlement; covered expense types; association membership count; expense-statement and receipt workflow; tax note; signed acknowledgment.

## When to use
- Funding seller association dues, subscriptions, journals, or conference fees/travel.
- Separating personal PD reimbursement from company-run training budgets.
- Needing a clear approval and finance submission path.

## What it covers
Purpose (encourage current professional development); policy (reimburse approved personal PD fees in addition to company courses); eligibility (full-time 3+ months; fees tied to role); entitlement ($(XX)/year; dues/subscriptions/journals; conference fees including travel/expenses; contact for eligibility questions; possible taxable reimbursement); process (company covers N association memberships/year; expense statement + original receipts to named approver then finance); acknowledgment.
