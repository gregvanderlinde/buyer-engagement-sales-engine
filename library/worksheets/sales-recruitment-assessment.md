# Sales Recruitment Assessment
Score how well the company hires sellers -- and where the process fails.

## Bottom line
Helps sales leaders evaluate hiring performance on a scorecard so weak steps in recruitment get fixed before the next costly miss.

## When to use
- Hire quality or time-to-productivity is slipping.
- After several regrettable hires.
- Building a recruiting improvement plan with HR.

## What it covers
Sheets: Overview, Instructions, Sales Recruitment Scorecard, Results. Importance per evaluation criterion; organization scored 1-6 on scorecard questions; Results show weighted outcomes and very low areas to address immediately.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Set criterion importance.
2. Score the recruitment scorecard 1-6.
3. Use Results to pick improvement focus.
