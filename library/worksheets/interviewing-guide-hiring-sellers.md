# Interviewing Guide for Hiring Sellers
Run a structured seller interview: prepare, open, behave-based questions, score, then evaluate.

## Bottom line
Helps sales leaders prepare, open, and question candidates across Introductory, Sales, Experience, and Personality perspectives, then score into Candidate Evaluation.

## When to use
- You are hiring sellers and need a consistent interview process.
- Interviewers must use behavioral (situation/objective/action/result) questions.
- You will score candidates on the Candidate Evaluation worksheet afterward.

## What it covers
Prepare: review questions and resume, note gaps, set uninterrupted location, gather materials. Open: introductions, candidate knowledge of role/company, process overview, expectations, clarifications. During: rapport, resume review, notes, body language, close with follow-up timing. Question areas: Introductory; Sales Perspective (skills, previous performance); Experience Perspective (industry, prior sales, training, academic); Personality Perspective (sales aptitude, communication, interpersonal). After: final notes, evaluate, use evaluation guide, enter scores into Candidate Evaluation. Full teaching depth: library/entries/interviewing-guide-hiring-sellers.md

## How to run it (seller)
1. Review the Full Library Entry and pick questions for this role.
2. Prepare from the resume; open with rapport and process clarity.
3. Ask behavioral questions (situation, objective, action, result); probe; avoid closed questions except confirmations.
4. After the interview, score into Candidate Evaluation; do not invent scores.
