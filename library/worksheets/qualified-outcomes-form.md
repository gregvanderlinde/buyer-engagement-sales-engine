# Qualified Outcomes Form
Capture the match factors and outcomes on one working page.

## Bottom line
One form for Business Objectives, Key Projects Prioritized, Concerns and Challenges, Current Solution, Unique Capabilities, Qualified Outcomes (The Numbers x The Win), Key Stakeholders, and Prove-to-Me Plan -- so you can see which factor is still zero.

## When to use
- Pre-call planning for an active opportunity.
- Live capture during discovery or validation calls.
- Post-call review and forecast coaching.
- Account planning when tying projects to larger Business Objectives.

## What it covers
Header: company, contacts, meeting date, short mission or general benefit statement for your offer.

Planning checks: pre-call prep done; basic account info filled; research questions prepared; org chart request; team size and budget context; consultants or advisory firms in use; strengths and weaknesses of their team; if existing client, renew-or-not and why.

Match factors (Unique Capability Match):
- Business Objectives -- what the business is trying to achieve; drivers.
- Business Issues -- what stands in the way; what must be done to get there.
- Key Projects Prioritized -- infrastructure / applications / other lanes; timeline.
- Concerns and Challenges to Overcome -- holding them back or near-future obstacles.
- Current Solution -- how they plan to address it in their eyes.
- Unique Capabilities -- how you help in ways others cannot; visualize differentiated solution.

Questioning lanes on each factor: Wide Open | 2nd and 3rd Level (explore impact / probe) | Validate.
Sample moves: Tell me about your current initiatives; What is driving this; So what I am hearing is; Can you tell me more about; Why is this occurring now; Help me understand; What are you experiencing; Tie to Business Objectives and Business Issues.

Qualified Outcomes section:
- The Numbers -- quantify business impact (time, money, risk, productivity).
- The Win -- personal motive framed for method use (credibility, look-good solving the issue, career).
- Key Stakeholders -- who is impacted, sponsor, budget, sign-off, veto.
- Prove-to-Me Plan -- what they need to see to be convinced.

Deal readiness reminder (multiplication): Qualified Outcomes Match x Qualified Outcomes x Key Stakeholders x Prove-to-Me Plan. A zero factor means not ready.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Fill what research already proved; leave honest blanks.
2. On the call, work left-to-right: objectives and projects, then concerns and current solution, then capabilities.
3. After each factor, validate in their words before moving on.
4. Add Numbers and Win only after the match sketch is real.
5. End with stakeholders to multi-thread and a Prove-to-Me ask for the next step.
6. Score zeros before you update the Buyer Engagement Forecast.
