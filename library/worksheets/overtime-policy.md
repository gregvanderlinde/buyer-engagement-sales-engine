# Overtime Policy
Govern eligibility and compensation for overtime, including FLSA-oriented definitions, approval rules, caps, pay vs compensatory leave, and manager acknowledgment.

## Bottom line
Defines overtime, exempt vs non-exempt placeholders, pre-approval and weekly caps, pay multiplier and compensatory leave rules, discretionary rewards -- must be localized to current labor law by counsel/HR; not legal advice.

## When to use
- Non-exempt staff may work beyond the standard workweek.
- Managers need a shared rule for budgeting, approving, and recording overtime.
- Exempt roles may receive hour-for-hour compensatory leave when pre-approved.

## What it covers
Purpose; HR and manager responsibility; definitions (FLSA overview placeholders, overtime, non-exempt, exempt examples including sellers/managers/admin/computer/creative, compensatory leave); policy (mandatory OT with notice when possible; budgeted pre-approval for non-exempt; weekly hour cap placeholder; pay rate multiplier for hours over XX; exempt compensatory leave hour-for-hour when Department Head pre-approves; no converting approved comp leave to OT pay; discretionary OT rewards with named pre-approval); signed acknowledgment.
