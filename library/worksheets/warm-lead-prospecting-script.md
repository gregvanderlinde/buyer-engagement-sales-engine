# Warm Lead Prospecting Script
Re-engage a warm lead from a prior touch without assuming the need.

## Bottom line
Six-part warm script: prior contact, confirm known information, benefit after discovery, relevant past results only, next steps with homework, and book the next meeting.

## When to use
- The prospect filled a form, visited a booth, or briefly met you before.
- You need clarification before proposing options.
- You want to avoid phone tag by booking the next time now.

## What it covers
Blocks: (1) State previous point of contact; (2) Recap/confirm known information (keep short) or ask clarification questions; (3) Benefit to client after questioning/discovery; (4) Past experience/success only when it specifically relates; (5) Next steps -- do homework and return with specific options; (6) Contact information -- confirm a time for the next meeting.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Open with the prior touchpoint in one sentence.
2. Confirm or clarify their stated interest before pitching.
3. Promise specific homework and book the next meeting time.
4. Use only relevant results; skip generic credibility filler.
