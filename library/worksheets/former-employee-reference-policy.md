# Former Employee Reference Policy
Route employment references for former sellers through HR, limit disclosed facts, and protect the company and departing employees from inappropriate reference risk.

## Bottom line
Centralizes references in HR; lists what may and may not be shared; optional release from departing sellers; written request preference -- not a substitute for counsel.

## When to use
- Managers or peers are asked for references on former sellers.
- HR needs a consistent, signed acknowledgment of who may speak and what may be said.
- Departing sellers may sign a release governing what HR can share.

## What it covers
Reason/goal (fair treatment + liability protection); scope (all sellers); policy points: HR-only references; allowed facts (name, titles, dates, salary ranges, documented signed performance, documented dangerous conduct leading to termination); prohibited content (unsupported opinions, non-job info, unsolicited job info except dangerous-behavior notice); optional exit release -- without it, confirm only name/titles/dates; prefer written reference requests; oral or written responses with confidentiality notice; no blank letters; refuse if requester lacks former employee permission; acknowledgment signature blocks.
