# Bid or No-Bid Assessment
Decide whether an RFP is worth the bid effort before you spend the team.

## Bottom line
Helps a sales leader weigh internal fit, buyer fit, risk, and economics so the team only bids where it can win and deliver.

## When to use
- An RFP or bid invitation lands and resources are limited.
- Leadership wants a shared, scored reason to bid or walk.
- You need a shortlist of issues to fix before a bid kickoff.

## What it covers
Sheets include Overview, Instructions, Bid or No-Bid Assessment, and Results. You weight category importance so totals equal 100% across Internal, Buyer/External, Risk, and Financial/Economic assessment. Each criteria row is scored 1-6 (strongly disagree to strongly agree), with flags for bid-prevention issues and comments. Sample criteria cover mandate alignment, leadership support, delivery capability, buyer relationships, budget approval, and risk items. Results summarize category scores, bid fit by weight, weaknesses to address before bidding, and major issues.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Copy the spreadsheet for this opportunity.
2. Set category importance weights (must total 100%).
3. Score each criterion 1-6; mark bid-prevention issues and comments.
4. Read the Results sheet for strengths, weaknesses, and major issues -- do not edit Results.
5. Original remains a spreadsheet; this card is the library guide only.
