# Seller Health Check Scorecard
Track account health by seller across review cycles, then act on the weak accounts.

## Bottom line
Helps sales leaders review account health per seller at set intervals so coaching focuses on accounts that need care, cross-sell, or recovery.

## When to use
- Quarterly or monthly account reviews with sellers.
- You need a repeatable definition of a healthy account.
- Comparing health trends across multiple review sessions.

## What it covers
Sheets: Overview, Instructions, Input, Review 1-5, Summary. Editable account evaluation criteria and health scoring ranges; accounts entered and scored per review cycle. Health Index style scoring drives action notes. Summary compiles results across prior review sessions for trend comparison.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Copy the workbook per team or cycle.
2. Edit evaluation criteria, point values, and health ranges on Input.
3. Enter accounts; complete a Review sheet per session.
4. Use Summary to compare sessions and decide actions with each seller.
