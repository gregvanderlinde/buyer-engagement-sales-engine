# Three-Month Moving Average Forecaster
Compare forecasted dollars and activity milestones to actuals.

## Bottom line
Helps sales leaders see forecast accuracy for dollars and key activities so coaching and planning follow the gaps.

## When to use
- Weekly or monthly forecast hygiene reviews.
- Comparing seller or team forecast versus actual.
- Checking whether activity milestones track with revenue.

## What it covers
Sheets: Overview, Instructions, Forecasting Tool, Results. Enter forecasted dollars and sales activity milestones per seller or department; enter actuals for milestones and dollars. Results compare forecast vs actual (graph for dollars; table for percent achieved on dollars and actions).

## How to run it (seller)
1. Copy the sheet for the period.
2. Enter forecast dollars and milestone counts; later enter actuals.
3. Read Results for percent achieved and the dollar comparison graph.
