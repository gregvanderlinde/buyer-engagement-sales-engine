# Blackberry / Mobile Device Policy
Govern acquisition, use, security, and support of company or approved personal Blackberry / mobile devices used by sellers to access corporate systems.

## Bottom line
Defines eligibility, approved use, device and security rules, IT ownership, loss/theft reporting, and a signed acknowledgment -- not legal advice; route final language through HR/IT/legal.

## When to use
- Sellers need company email or systems access from a Blackberry or other approved mobile device.
- IT must control install, configuration, antivirus, password, VPN/firewall, and timeout settings.
- Leadership wants a signed policy before issuing or approving devices.

## What it covers
Purpose and scope (sellers accessing corporate resources via mobile); eligibility and management approval; use rules (secure email, approved locations, limited browsing/attachments at IT discretion); device rules (deactivate on risk, personal devices need written IT/management approval, report loss/theft, procurement compliance, dispose damaged devices per policy); security (antivirus, password policy, remote firewall/VPN, inactivity timeouts, no sensitive data storage beyond privacy rules); support and monitoring via enterprise server; acknowledgment with employee and manager signature blocks. Placeholders: company name, carrier, timeout minutes/hours, employee name.
