# Qualified Outcomes Cheat Sheets
Questioning levels and discovery reminders for Qualified Outcomes calls.

## Bottom line
Three questioning moves -- Wide Open, 2nd/3rd Level, Validate -- plus must-have project info and a discovery-call skeleton that keeps you out of survey mode.

## When to use
- Any discovery or validation conversation using Qualified Outcomes.
- Coaching when calls sound like checklists instead of dialogue.
- Pre-call warm-up for the first five minutes.

## What it covers

### Wide Open (guide / control)
Open probes encourage the buyer to respond freely. They start dialogue and give you time to listen. They set tone and direction and get the buyer talking about themselves.

### 2nd and 3rd Level (explore impact / probe)
Clarify. Deepen understanding. Uncover more needs or the real reason behind the open talk. Should sound conversational and get to the heart of the matter. Without these, the conversation is shallow and sounds like a survey.

### Validate
Solicit feedback. Confirm and clarify. Understand -- do not assume. Align to the buyer's confirmed explanation. There is no room for assumptions; only actual needs close deals.

### Key Projects Prioritized -- must-have information
Priority. Timeline. Stage, budget, and scope when relevant. Tie projects to Business Objectives.

### Discovery-call skeleton
1. Business Objectives line (write what you hear).
2. Unique Capability Match questions for projects, concerns, current solution, capabilities.
3. If you delivered research: how did it help them do the job better; quantify impact; what it means to company and to them.
4. Stakeholders and Prove-to-Me ask when earned.
5. Book validation -- value confirmation -- as the next step that hardens the match.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Open wide for at least a few turns before you deepen.
2. After every deep cluster, validate in their words.
3. Capture Key Projects Prioritized must-haves before you leave the topic.
4. Use the Prompter for factor coverage; use this sheet for questioning craft.
