# Scheduled Break Policy
Define meal breaks and paid scheduled breaks for sellers on a regular workday, plus religious accommodation flexibility and compliance consequences.

## Bottom line
Unpaid meal break duration placeholder; paid scheduled break count/duration/times for an 8-hour day; reasonable religious prayer accommodation via manager/HR; unscheduled breaks without authorization can lead to discipline.

## When to use
- Inside or shift-based seller teams needing published break schedules.
- Exceeding or documenting break practice relative to labor minimums.
- Handling religious accommodation requests for prayer timing.

## What it covers
Purpose (scheduled breaks exceeding DOL minimum placeholders); meal breaks (XX minutes, scheduled, unpaid, no work required); scheduled breaks (count and duration for 8-hour day, paid as time worked, stated times); religious accommodation (reasonable efforts; contact manager/HR; flexible breaks case-by-case); compliance (unauthorized unscheduled breaks -> discipline up to termination); acknowledgment.
