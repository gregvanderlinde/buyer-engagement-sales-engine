# Understand Yourself Self-Assessment
A CPRD-style self-read so you see your natural tendencies before you adapt.

## Bottom line
Helps sellers and sales leaders mark natural preferences (yes/no on adjective prompts) and see which CPRD-aligned pattern the Results sheet highlights.

## When to use
- Starting CPRD practice for yourself or a team workshop.
- Comparing personal style to role requirements.
- Before coaching on adapting to buyer styles.

## What it covers
Sheets: Overview, Instructions, Self Assessment, Results. Answer each adjective prompt Yes or No from the dropdown in order, first reaction. Results highlight the matching personality category; ties highlight each tied section. Source framing: no right/wrong answers; natural tendencies only. Ties to CPRD library terms.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Copy the workbook for personal use.
2. Answer every prompt Yes/No in order without overthinking.
3. Read Results highlighting; pair with term:cprd and knowing-yourself materials.
