# Travel Expense Policy
Guidelines for seller business travel outside day-to-day local expenses: pre-authorization, lodging, meals/entertainment, transport, additional expenses, expense reports, and reimbursement.

## Bottom line
Pre-approve travel; justify vs cheaper alternatives; receipt thresholds; no spouse/family costs; lodging/meals/transport rules; additional reimbursables; submit report within N days; signed agreement accepting non-reimbursable costs.

## When to use
- Sellers travel regionally or overnight for clients/prospects.
- Managers approve trips and expense reports.
- Finance needs consistent receipt and reimbursement rules.

## What it covers
Purpose/scope (out-of-ordinary business travel; arrangers and approvers); manager duties (auth before commit; receipt over $X; justify vs webinar/teleconference; budget funds); prior to travel (supervisor approval; authorization form fields; no spouse/companion coverage; personal combo trips reimburse business portion only); expenses (reimburse after report; corporate card or employee float); lodging guidelines; meals/entertainment (no alcohol in template; client meal report fields); transportation (coach default, upgrades need prior approval; rental insurance; personal mileage rate; no fine reimbursement); additional (calls, parking/tolls, taxi/shuttle/subway, laundry/valet, optional per diem); expense report timing and advances; reimbursement schedule placeholder; agreement.
