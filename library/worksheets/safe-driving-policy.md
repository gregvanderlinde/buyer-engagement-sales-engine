# Safe Driving Policy
Guidelines for sellers operating company or personal vehicles on business: traffic laws, speeding, alcohol/drugs, mobile-phone ban, maintenance, weather, kits, and accident response -- plus an internal communicating-while-driving notice.

## Bottom line
Valid license and insurance, seatbelts, no impaired driving, no handheld phone/PDA use while driving, maintenance and weather rules, first-aid/road kits, accident notification, signed acknowledgment, and companion all-employee notice on communicating while driving.

## When to use
- Sellers drive company or personal vehicles for business.
- Reinforcing bans on handheld calling/texting/emailing while driving.
- After incidents or when rolling out vehicle safety expectations.

## What it covers
Purpose; representative responsibilities (follow policy, report H&S concerns, obey rules, use safety equipment, report hazards/incidents); rules of the road (license, insurance, traffic laws, courteous driving, seatbelts, working lights); speeding; alcohol/narcotics/fatigue and taxi/alternate travel support; mobile phones (no handheld; minimize hands-free); vehicle maintenance; inclement weather and lodging support when unsafe; first aid / road safety kits (company vehicles required; personal vehicles encouraged); accident steps (authorities, exchange info, notify manager); acknowledgment; internal communication memo on communicating while driving with discipline and cost-recovery language.
