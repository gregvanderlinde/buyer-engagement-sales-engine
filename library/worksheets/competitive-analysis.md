# Competitive Analysis
Score your company and named competitors the way buyers see them.

## Bottom line
Helps sales leaders map relative strengths and weaknesses versus competitors so sellers can build clearer differentiation.

## When to use
- Entering a new segment or refreshing win themes.
- Coaching sellers who lose on vague 'they are cheaper/better' objections.
- Leadership wants a shared competitive picture, not tribal knowledge.

## What it covers
Sheets: Overview, Instructions, Competitive Analysis, Results. You list your company plus competitors and score perception-in-the-buyer's-mind style criteria 1-6 with comments. Criteria samples include brand leadership, quality leadership, product development leadership, and seller strength. Results total scores across companies for comparison.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Copy the sheet; review criteria; enter competitor names.
2. Score your company and each competitor 1-6 with comments.
3. Review Results totals; turn gaps into differentiation coaching for sellers.
