# Referral Prospecting Script
Open with the referrer, state the reason, and ask for a short fit meeting.

## Bottom line
Five-part referral script: greeting with referral context, reason for contact without assumptions, next-step transition, relevant past results, and current industry context.

## When to use
- A satisfied client or peer suggested a contact.
- You want a warmer open than a pure cold call.
- You need a consistent referral dial structure.

## What it covers
Blocks: (1) Greeting and referral information quickly (name, company, role, who suggested contact); (2) Reason for contact -- no assumptions; propose time to discuss fit; (3) Next steps / transition to a short meeting; (4) Past experience/success with similar firms when relevant; (5) Relative current industry information.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Confirm permission/context with the referrer when required.
2. Lead with the referral name and a clear, short ask.
3. Use only results that match this buyer's situation.
4. Book the next conversation time before hanging up when possible.
