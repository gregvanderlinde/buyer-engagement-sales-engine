# Goal Issue Worksheet
Drill from a buyer goal into reasons, scaled questions, and numerical answers.

## Bottom line
Pre-meeting brainstorm of possible reasons, then during/post-meeting capture of goal, reason, diagnostic questions, and buyer answers so later presentations stay fact-based.

## When to use
- Discovery needs a systematic goal-to-issue drill-down.
- You want numerical facts at a scale the buyer can answer.
- You are preparing a consultative conversation before pitching.

## What it covers
Pre-Meeting: Goal, Name and Title, Possible Reasons (1-4). During/Post-Meeting: Goal, Name and Title, Reason, Questions paired with Buyer's Answers. Guidance: brainstorm typical issues for similar titles/industries; ask diagnostic questions in smaller units; let the buyer self-discover financial implications.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Before the meeting, estimate the likely goal and possible reasons.
2. In-meeting, pick the real reason and ask scaled diagnostic questions.
3. Record numerical answers; do not invent metrics.
4. Use the completed sheet to drive a fact-based follow-up or proposal.
