# Negotiation Worksheet
Prepare give-and-take before late-stage price pressure hits.

## Bottom line
Prepares readiness checks, topics (value, cost, terms), tactics, and phrase banks so you give only when you get something of equal value.

## When to use
- Late-stage buyer pushback on price or terms is likely.
- You need a give/take map before the negotiation meeting.
- You want phrases that re-anchor to agreed value and business issues.

## What it covers
Readiness questions: goals understood; personal and business value clear; differentiation validated; can finish today; buying authority; final request. Negotiate topics: personal/business value; cost benefit and risk/reward; terms (price, payment, contract, delivery) with reference accounts when giving something away. Tactics: action confirmation questions; wait; remind of value and prior concessions; never give early without return. Phrase banks: general if/then; agreed-upon plan; dollar payback; business issue; business goal.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Answer the readiness questions honestly before the meeting.
2. List what you can give and what you must take, with values.
3. Pick phrase banks that match the pressure you expect.
4. In-meeting, confirm action, wait, and trade -- do not invent concessions.
