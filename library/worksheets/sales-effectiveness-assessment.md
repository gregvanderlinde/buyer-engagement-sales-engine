# Sales Effectiveness Assessment
Evaluate sales department effectiveness so priorities follow the evidence.

## Bottom line
Helps sales leaders systematically score key effectiveness areas and see weighted results plus concern flags for where to improve.

## When to use
- Quarterly effectiveness reviews.
- Prioritizing coaching or process work after a weak quarter.
- Comparing self-assessment views across leaders.

## What it covers
Sheets: Overview, Instructions, Sales Department Effectiveness, Results. Importance set per evaluation criterion; organization scored 1-6 per question; Results show weighted outcomes and areas of concern.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Set importance on the effectiveness tab.
2. Score 1-6 per question.
3. Read Results for weighted picture and concern areas.
