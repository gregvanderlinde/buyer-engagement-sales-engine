# Value Proposition Worksheet for Prospecting
Craft a short, fact-based prospecting value proposition that earns a next conversation.

## Bottom line
Build industry- and title-specific value props from documented facts, numbers, and time frames -- buyer Desired Outcomes and MBOs first, not product features. Ban feature-first intros.

## When to use
- You have roughly 30-60 seconds to earn attention when prospecting.
- You need more than one value prop keyed to unique capabilities.
- Complex sales need higher specificity than transactional ones.
- Gap / Qualified (Golden) Wedge promotion needs MBO + toward/away language.

## Required fields (5078 / 5078a)
Fill before writing the prop. Blank OK until [FOUND] / [CONFIRM]. Never invent.

| Field | Intent | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **MBO** | Management by Objective for this seat / segment -- how they are measured | blank / draft / confirmed |
| **Desired Outcome -- toward** | What they want true that is not true today (their nouns) | blank / draft / confirmed |
| **Desired Outcome -- away** | What they want to avoid / stop / not risk (their nouns) | blank / draft / confirmed |
| **mbo_constraint** (optional) | Timing, budget window, board date, harvest constraint | blank / draft / confirmed |

Minimum wedge shape: `Current State (their words) -> Desired Outcome toward and/or away (their words) + MBO/constraint (their measure) -- confirmed?`

## What it covers
Must include: documented facts and assumptions; numbers ($, %, FTE, etc.); time frames; MBO + toward/away Desired Outcomes; only relevant buyer benefits; clear transition to the buyer's status. Must **not** open with company/product/features/process maps -- open in their issues, deals moving, questions answered. Sections: Introduction (buyer-world first); Applicable Benefits Seen; How you will help the buyer's company (from pre-call research); transition to ask for time.

## Banned openers
- Feature / advantage / benefit stacks as the first line
- "We have templates / role cards / worksheets"
- Process-as-feature or packaging as the hero (packaging may appear later as what-it-is, never the lead)

## How to run it (seller)
1. Capture MBO + Desired Outcome toward/away (or mark unknown and ask).
2. Document the facts and assumptions behind each claim.
3. Write 2-3 sentences of intro in *their* world (issues / outcomes / measure), then quantified peer benefits, then a buyer-specific help statement.
4. Create multiple variants keyed to strengths and competitive differentiators.
5. Practice to time; drop anything that is not buyer-relevant or that leads with features.
