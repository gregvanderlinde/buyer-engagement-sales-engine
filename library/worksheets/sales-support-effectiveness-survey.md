# Sales Support Effectiveness Survey
Hear how sellers rate coaching, training, and support -- then prioritize fixes.

## Bottom line
Helps sales leaders capture seller perception of support resources so improvement work follows what sellers actually use and value.

## When to use
- Enablement investment needs a seller-voice baseline.
- Support functions disagree on what sellers need.
- After a tools or training rollout, to see uptake perception.

## What it covers
Sheets: Overview, Instructions, Sales Support Evaluation, Results. Importance per criterion; every question scored 1-6; Results show weighted strengths and focus areas. Framed as understanding seller perception of resources, coaching, and training.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Set importance weights.
2. Have raters (or a consolidated view) score each question 1-6.
3. Review Results graphs for strengths and gaps.
