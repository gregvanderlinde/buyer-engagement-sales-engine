# Qualified Outcomes Prompter
On-call reminder card for match factors and questioning lanes.

## Bottom line
A one-page prompter: Business Objectives across the top; Unique Capability Match factors in the middle; Qualified Outcomes, Key Stakeholders, and Prove-to-Me at the base -- each with Wide Open | 2nd/3rd Level | Validate.

## When to use
- Live discovery or validation calls when you need the spine visible.
- Coaching ride-alongs so both parties share one talk track.
- First-call prospecting when you risk drifting into a pitch.

## What it covers
Layout zones:
1. Business Objectives (write-in line).
2. Unique Capability Match: Key Projects Prioritized; Concerns and Challenges; Current Solution; Unique Capabilities.
3. Qualified Outcomes Match: The Numbers; The Win; Key Stakeholders; Prove-to-Me Plan.

On every zone: Wide Open | 2nd and 3rd Level Impact | Validate.

Starter prompts (adapt to their words):
- Projects: Tell me about your current initiatives; What is your timeline on this?
- Concerns: What issues do you need to overcome to accomplish...; What are you experiencing; What is causing...; Why is this occurring now; How might this impact...; Why are you concerned...
- Current Solution: How are you currently planning on tackling...; What alternatives have you looked at; From where you are today what do you require to achieve...
- Capabilities: What if you had the capability to...; Would these capabilities help address your objectives and issues?
- Numbers / Win: How would you quantify the impact; How much value is associated with overcoming these challenges; How does this affect you; What will this mean to your company; What will this mean to you.
- Stakeholders / proof: Who is impacted; Who is the business sponsor; Who else is involved; Who has budget; Who signs off; Who can veto; What do you need to see to be convinced.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Print or keep beside the Form; do not read it like a script.
2. Stay in Wide Open until they are talking freely.
3. Deepen only after you have something worth deepening.
4. Validate before you attach Unique Capabilities or outcomes math.
5. After the call, transfer notes to the Form and mark zero factors.
