# Company-Wide Issue Worksheet
See how one business issue lands differently across roles and departments.

## Bottom line
Tracks a vertical view of one company issue by name/role, issue, and reason so you can tailor the approach and align buyers.

## When to use
- Multiple departments feel the same business issue differently.
- Pre-call planning or post-meeting capture across a buying committee.
- You need to show how tactical issues lower in the org connect to corporate issues higher up.

## What it covers
Vertical worksheet: Name & Role, Issue, Reason -- repeated for each stakeholder. Guidance: lower in the company = more tactical; higher = more corporate. Used in pre-call planning and completed after client interactions. Example pattern shows owner, sales director, IT manager, programmer, and sales manager views of declining existing-client revenue.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Name the company and departments involved.
2. For each stakeholder, capture Name & Role, Issue, and Reason in their words.
3. Compare tactical vs corporate framing across levels.
4. Use the map to tailor questions and solution positioning per role.
