# Seller Severance Package Policy
Baseline severance expectations for involuntarily departing sellers (workforce reduction, performance, or conduct), including pay schedule, commissions timing, accruals, benefits, and caps -- subordinate to HR and statute.

## Bottom line
Documents severance amount by tenure, commission timing, accruals, benefits end date, unemployment note, and possible reduction if new employment is secured; does not replace statutory minimums or existing HR procedures.

## When to use
- Planning involuntary separations for sellers.
- Setting consistent tenure-based severance formulas and commission wind-down timing.
- Coordinating benefits end dates with payroll/HR.

## What it covers
Intro (baseline only; defer to HR/statute); purpose (RIF/performance/conduct; procedures, schedule, benefits); policy (severance for terminated employees; rate by tenure); schedule (regular salary rate; outstanding commissions paid 90 days after termination; accruals included; 2 weeks per year worked; max 36 weeks placeholder; lump sum or salary continuance choice); benefits (health/dental through last month of severance pay; unemployment note; package may reduce if new employment secured); acknowledgment.
