# Cold Call Prospecting Script
Build a cold-call script that earns agreement early and asks for a clear next step.

## Bottom line
Gives you a seven-part cold outreach script: identity, early agreement, cold-call acknowledgement, reason, context, buyer benefit, and a timed next step.

## When to use
- You are opening net-new conversations with no prior relationship.
- You want a repeatable cold-call structure rather than improvising every dial.
- Coaching needs a shared script skeleton for new sellers.

## What it covers
Required blocks: (1) Name, company, role; (2) True-for-person opener to earn early agreement; (3) Acknowledge it is a cold call; (4) Reason for contact (company truth or peer benefits); (5) Context (referral or industry peers when appropriate); (6) Purpose with clear buyer benefit; (7) Next steps -- stick to the call intent and ask for a short meeting to test fit. Example lines are placeholders only; customize with real facts.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Fill each of the seven blocks with verified facts for this buyer.
2. Keep the ask specific (minutes requested and purpose).
3. Practice aloud; drop filler and hype words.
4. Log the outcome and refine the blocks that fail most often.
