# Decision Matrix
Weight criteria, score options, and take emotion out of hard allocation calls.

## Bottom line
Helps sales leaders compare decisions or options on weighted criteria so resource and budget calls rest on facts instead of volume of opinion.

## When to use
- Two or more initiatives compete for the same budget or headcount.
- A decision needs a transparent trail for leadership.
- Stakeholders disagree and need a shared scoring frame.

## What it covers
Sheets: Overview, Instructions, Decision Matrix Tool, Decision Items, Results. Up to five weighted criteria, each with questions; options or decision items are scored and rolled into Results. Weights and scores drive a ranked outcome without requiring the user to invent formulas outside the sheet.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Copy the workbook.
2. Enter up to five criteria and weight them; add questions under each.
3. List decision items and score them.
4. Read Results for the ranked outcome.
