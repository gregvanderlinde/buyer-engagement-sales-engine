# Branding Efforts Prioritization
Rank branding and advertising options by sales, economic, and company fit.

## Bottom line
Helps sales leaders choose which branding or advertising options deserve budget by scoring them against weighted criteria.

## When to use
- Marketing and sales must pick among awareness channels under a fixed budget.
- You need a shared ranking of branding options, not a gut call.
- Options span classic and digital channels and need the same scorecard.

## What it covers
Sheets: Overview, Instructions, Advertising Evaluation, Scoring, Rankings. Criteria groups cover Sales Perspective (lead generation, awareness, differentiation), Economic Perspective (budget, existing technology, ROI), and Company Perspective (fits value, existing skills, target market). Importance weights must total 100%. Options are scored 1-6 on each criterion. Sample option rows include newspapers, radio, television, yellow pages, brochures, coupons, white papers, trade shows, blogs, and search ads. Rankings show weighted results by perspective and overall.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Copy the sheet; edit criteria and importance weights (100% total).
2. List the branding options under consideration on Scoring.
3. Score each option 1-6 per criterion.
4. Read Rankings for weighted order; do not type into result cells.
