# Sales Leader Effort Prioritization
Choose where a sales leader's time and projects will move results.

## Bottom line
Helps sales leaders compare tactical projects and initiatives on weighted criteria so effort lands where impact is highest.

## When to use
- A leader's calendar is full of low-leverage work.
- Picking among coaching, process, and project options.
- Team capacity is tight and initiatives must be ranked.

## What it covers
Sheets: Overview, Instructions, Sales Effort Prioritization, Scoring, Rankings. Set importance of selection criteria; edit projects/initiatives; score 1-6; Rankings show weighted scores.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Set criterion importance.
2. Edit initiatives on Scoring; score 1-6.
3. Use Rankings to decide what to keep, defer, or drop.
