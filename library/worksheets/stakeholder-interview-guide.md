# Stakeholder Interview Guide
Consistent talking points for internal stakeholder interviews that surface alignment gaps.

## Bottom line
Conversation guide (not a rigid script) covering introduction, mandates, results, initiatives, cross-department interaction, alignment gaps, and wrap-up.

## When to use
- Sales leaders need aligned views across departments.
- You are priming an RFP or major initiative and need consistent stakeholder input.
- Alignment gaps between departments are unclear.

## What it covers
Topics: Introduction; Business Mandates; Department Results; Initiatives; Department Interaction; Alignment Gaps; Wrap up. Sample prompts include goals/outcomes, process support for capabilities, system requirements, interaction with sales and other areas, top three alignment changes and benefits, summary read-back, who else to speak with, and next-step willingness.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Use as talking points; keep question order consistent across stakeholders.
2. Capture answers in the stakeholder's words.
3. Read back top problems to confirm understanding.
4. Ask who else to interview and whether they will join next steps.
