# Personal Cell Phone Use Policy
Control personal cell phone and PDA use for inside sellers during work hours to protect focus, customer experience, and confidential company information.

## Bottom line
Phones off/silenced during work unless management-approved; personal calls on breaks or emergency with approval; ban on texting, storage/photos of company info, and non-business entertainment features; signed acknowledgment.

## When to use
- Inside sales or open-floor environments where personal phones interrupt customers or teammates.
- Prohibiting cameras/storage of confidential business data on personal devices.
- Setting emergency and break-period exceptions.

## What it covers
Purpose (personal cell/Blackberry use); scope (inside sellers); policy (prohibit personal use in regular hours; exceptions by management; no storing company info or taking pictures); procedures (calls on breaks; emergency only with approval; no personal texting; silence/off unless business-approved; no MP3/mobile-internet/games on personal phones during work; no camera/memory removal of confidential info; no illegal/harassing use on company or personal business phones per code of conduct); acknowledgment signature blocks.
