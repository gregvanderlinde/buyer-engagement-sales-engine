# Qualified Outcomes Question Bank
Sample questions by match factor -- guidelines, not a script.

## Bottom line
A merged bank for Business Objectives, projects, issues, current solution, differentiation, Numbers, Win, and stakeholders. Use as prompts; put every question in the buyer's language.

## When to use
- Building a talk track before a discovery or validation call.
- Filling gaps on the Form when a factor is still zero.
- Coaching sellers who need better second- and third-level questions.

## What it covers

### Business Objectives
- We both know projects get prioritized by business goals. What business goal or objective is driving this initiative?
- Who is driving this business goal? Are they also the business sponsor?
- What are your company's strategic goals for the upcoming year? How does this relate to your functional objectives?

### Key Projects Prioritized
- What are your top 3-5 initiatives currently?
- Which of these are most relevant to you right now? What else?
- What stage are you at with this project? Have you dedicated internal resources? Who, full time or part time?
- Priorities? Timeline? Next major milestone? On schedule?
- Budget? On budget? What is the budget?
- Scope? Do you find staying on scope is a pressing issue?
- As far as time, scope, and budget, are you on track? How does coming in over on any of these affect you?

### Concerns and Challenges / Issues
- What are some of the challenges you foresee on this project?
- What do you need to overcome in order to accomplish this on time, budget, and scope?
- Why are you experiencing this now? What is causing this?
- How is this going to affect you? What will this mean to you?
- Which of your projects is causing you the most concern?
- How might this impact the project, you, or the business?
- What is wrong with the current process for...?
- What would happen if you chose the wrong approach or hit a major delay?
- What are the consequences or impact of a major unforeseen problem?

### Current Solution
- From where you are today, what do you require to accomplish this?
- What do you have in place now for...?
- How are you currently planning on tackling...?
- What gaps do you feel there may be in your next-steps strategy?
- What alternatives have you looked at?
- Are you using outside consultants or vendors? How do you manage them?

### Differentiation / Unique Capabilities
- What if you had the capability to...?
- Would these capabilities help you address your Business Objectives and Business Issues?
- Have you thought about...?
- What do you need to see to be convinced?
- I have ideas for how to demonstrate our capabilities -- I would like your feedback first.

### The Numbers (business result)
- How would you quantify the impact -- time, money, risk?
- How much value is associated with overcoming these challenges?
- What will this mean to your company?
- How important is this to the business?
- What is lost by not taking action?
- How much risk has been mitigated -- or could be?
- Can you estimate how much time or money you would save?

### The Win (personal -- method use; do not label in outbound buyer copy)
- How does this affect you?
- What will this mean to you?
- How did this help you accomplish your job (tasks and activities) better?

### Key Stakeholders / Prove-to-Me
- Who is impacted by this decision?
- Who is the business sponsor for this project?
- Who else is involved in this decision or project?
- Who else has budget to help get this done?
- Who signs off? Who can veto?
- What do you need to see to be convinced?

### Open-ended craft reminders
Prefer who / what / when / where / how / why / tell me about / help me understand. Avoid yes-no traps until you are validating. Wide open first; deepen; validate.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Pick 5-8 questions that match the zero factors on your Form -- not the whole bank.
2. Rewrite each in the buyer's vocabulary from research.
3. Sequence: objectives and projects, then issues and current solution, then capabilities, then outcomes and stakeholders.
4. After the call, retire questions that felt survey-like; keep the ones that opened dialogue.
