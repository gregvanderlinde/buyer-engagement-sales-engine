# Seller Referral Policy
Employee referral program for hiring sellers: eligibility, conflict exclusions, submission process, tenure gate before bonus payout, and role-based bonus rates.

## Bottom line
All employees may refer; bonus after referred hire completes XX months; excludes family, prior/current temps/employees, and referrals into the referrer's reporting line; HR form + resume required; tax note.

## When to use
- Posting seller or sales-management roles and inviting employee referrals.
- Paying referral bonuses with clear eligibility and conflict rules.
- Prioritizing employee-referred candidates in screening.

## What it covers
Purpose (referral program + monetary bonus on successful hire); posting locations via HR; employee eligibility (all employees; unlimited referrals); referral eligibility (qualified, not previously affiliated); non-eligible (reports to referrer; listed family/domestic relations; current/former temp or full-time employees); process (resume + HR referral form; priority over non-referred; outcome notice; bonus after XX months; taxes; management final disputes); rates for management vs individual-contributor roles $(XX); acknowledgment.
