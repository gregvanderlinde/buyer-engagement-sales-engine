# Email Acceptable Use Policy
Define appropriate and prohibited use of company email systems for sellers and other account holders, including monitoring, retention, and sanctions.

## Bottom line
Covers account activation/termination, end-user expectations, appropriate vs inappropriate use, monitoring/confidentiality, misuse reporting, disclaimer, failure-to-comply sanctions, and a signed user agreement -- guidance only; counsel finalizes jurisdiction-specific language.

## When to use
- Issuing company email accounts to sellers or approved third parties.
- Clarifying personal-use limits, attachment/virus caution, and password rules.
- Documenting the company's right to monitor, archive, and retrieve email under policy and law.

## What it covers
Purpose and scope (owned systems, users, records); account activation/termination and third-party eligibility placeholders; expectations (timely checking, mailbox hygiene, professional tone); appropriate use (business communication, information sharing, professional development); inappropriate use (illegal activity, policy violations, unauthorized access, unsafe attachments, password sharing, excessive personal/mass/political/commercial use); monitoring and confidentiality (IT/legal/management review, backups/archives, retention XX years placeholder); reporting misuse; disclaimer; sanctions (revoke access, discipline, termination, legal action); signature blocks.
