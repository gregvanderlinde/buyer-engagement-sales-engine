# Lead Generation Maturity Assessment
Score how mature your lead generation engine is -- and where it breaks.

## Bottom line
Helps sales leaders assess company lead-generation capability across weighted categories so under-performing areas get clear improvement focus.

## When to use
- Pipeline quality is thin and blame is split between marketing and sales.
- Planning a lead-gen improvement project.
- You need a baseline maturity picture before investing.

## What it covers
Sheets: Overview, Instructions, Lead Generation Assessment, Results. Category importance must total 100%. Organization scored 1-6 per question. Results graph strengths and gaps and flag significant issues to address immediately.

## How to run it (seller)
1. Copy the workbook; set category importance to 100% total.
2. Score each question 1-6 on the assessment sheet.
3. Review Results for strengths, gaps, and urgent issues.
