# Laptop Use Agreement Policy
Short signed acceptance of responsibility for a company laptop: care, business-only use, maintenance, return on exit, and repair/replacement cost.

## Bottom line
One-page assignment agreement tying laptop ID to the employee, replacement cost placeholder, business-use and policy cross-references, return-on-exit duty, and dual signatures.

## When to use
- Issuing a company laptop to a seller or other employee.
- Documenting financial responsibility for loss, theft, or damage.
- Offboarding when the device must be returned immediately.

## What it covers
Named acceptance of specific laptop ID; responsibility for repair/replacement at $(XX); business-only use; acknowledgment of internet/computer-use policies; maintenance updates per computer-use policy; immediate return in good working condition at end of employment for any reason; employee and manager signature/date blocks.
