The Competition Does Not Matter

Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on the competition does not matter.
Core~6 min
Executive summary

Sellers who employ a proactive sales strategy do not worry about the competition. Since they are in a position of power with the buyer, they force the competition to react to their solution. While there is value in understanding the competitive landscape, sellers must avoid mistakes by focusing on the buyer rather than on the competition. Focusing too much on competitors forces sellers to overcome their competitors' strengths rather than solely focusing on alignment with buyers. While it is important to acknowledge that there is competition, sellers need to focus on proactive alignment and demonstrate value to buyers in order to be more effective.

Bottom line

Sellers who employ a proactive sales strategy do not worry about the competition.
