Components of Effective Compensation Plans

Practical teaching for sellers and sales leaders on components of effective compensation plans.
Core~6 min
Executive summary

Most sales leaders are not fully aware of how effective their current compensation plan is. This is a major problem. Compensation plans, particularly those with a variable component, are designed to illicit desirable behaviors and/or outcomes from sellers. Paying sellers for results that were not the primary objectives for the company is wasted money. Setting objectives with clear related metrics attached will ensure that both the company and sellers reap the benefits of a solid compensation plan. Sales leaders must pay attention to the complexity and desired outcomes of their compensation plans. Failing to set objectives, accounting for territory and account variables, and creating an overly complex plan will diminish the effectiveness of a compensation plan, resulting in a financial liability for the company.

Bottom line

Most sales leaders are not fully aware of how effective their current compensation plan is.
