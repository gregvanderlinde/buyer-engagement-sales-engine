New Account Manager Introduction Script

Announce a seller change and introduce the new account owner without losing the relationship.
Corescript
Bottom line

Five-part script: announce the change, relevant past experience, personable commitment, contact/next steps, and appreciation for the relationship.

When to use

  • An account manager is promoted, reassigned, or departing.
  • Buyers need a clear handoff with contact paths.
  • You want consistent messaging for admin and leadership outreach.
What it covers

Blocks: (1) Announce Change (promotion or departure wording); (2) Relevant Past Experience of the incoming owner; (3) Positive/personable commitment statement; (4) Contact Information and Next Steps (who calls when; backup contacts); (5) Appreciative close tied to continued standards and loyalty.

How to run it

  • Choose promotion vs departure announce language that matches the facts.
  • Fill experience and contact details for the new owner.
  • Send or call per the account plan; copy stakeholders as needed.
  • Pair with Seller Departure Checklist when the prior owner is leaving.
