New Employee Cost Calculator

See the full one-time and ongoing cost of bringing a seller on board.
Corecalculator
Bottom line

Helps sales leaders track real hiring cost -- recruiting, setup, tools, and ongoing support -- not just salary.

When to use

  • Budgeting a new seller seat.
  • Comparing build-vs-buy or contractor alternatives.
  • Explaining true cost of headcount to finance.
What it covers

Sheets: Overview, Instructions, General Costs. Cost lines tagged One Time or Ongoing with descriptions: recruiting, initial training, ongoing training, office space/furniture, computers, software/licensing, communication devices and monthly fees, expense account, travel, and related items. Ongoing costs intended on an annual basis.

How to run it

  • Copy the sheet; edit General Costs to your reality.
  • Mark each line one-time or ongoing and enter dollar amounts.
  • Sum for the true cost of a new seller seat.
