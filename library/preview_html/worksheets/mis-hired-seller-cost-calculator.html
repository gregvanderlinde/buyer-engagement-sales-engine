Mis-Hired Seller Cost Calculator

Put a dollar range on the real cost of a bad seller hire.
Corecalculator
Bottom line

Helps leaders estimate one-time and ongoing costs of a mis-hire so hiring quality gets the investment it deserves.

When to use

  • Making the business case for better recruiting or onboarding.
  • After a failed hire, to capture total cost of the miss.
  • Comparing cost of a mis-hire versus investment in assessment.
What it covers

Sheets: Overview, Instructions, General Costs (openpyxl). Inputs include salary-linked defaults the user can edit: recruiting cost, signing bonus, severance, initial training, ongoing training, benefits, draw, lost sales, hardware, software/licensing, and related items. Descriptions explain each cost line; formulas in the source sheet compute from salary where defaults exist. This card does not restate specific formula coefficients as product claims -- edit the sheet for your numbers.

How to run it

  • Copy the workbook; open General Costs.
  • Enter or edit cost lines; mark amounts to match your company.
  • Read the rolled-up cost of the mis-hire; use it in hiring investment talks.
