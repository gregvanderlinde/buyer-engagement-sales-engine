## Audience

Owners and capable-but-not-professional sellers who have sold but never run a real process -- not absolute beginners.

## Ethos

**Buyer engagement and ask -- not sell and tell.** Engage the buyer, get curious, ask; never pitch-and-pray. Through-line: term:why-curiosity + term:curiosity-conversation.

## Free vs Run (read before the outline)

| Surface | What this path does |
| --- | --- |
| **Free (this repo)** | Serve **terms** and **worksheet fronts** under `library/`. Learn as you go. Artifact = notes / chat fills / preview cards. Do **not** open `_system/templates/...` -- that folder is not in Free. |
| **Run (paid)** | Same terms + full template kit bodies under `_system/templates/`. Boot, Seller Brief files, send gate, and completeness strip become live operating artifacts. |

When a step needs a full form: on Free, open the matching `library/worksheets/<name>.md` front and stop at **FREE PREVIEW ENDS HERE**. Say Run unlocks the complete worksheet body. Never invent paid file contents.
